## Put your AI to work on today’s marketing problem

“What actually makes this product worth buying?” “After ads and shipping, what do we keep?” “They bought once — how do we bring them back?”

**Marketing Skills for Everyone** gives you **365 complete Markdown method packs** to upload to an AI conversation. Each includes the inputs it needs, steps to follow, output fields and checks that help you assess the result

**Describe your problem and let the AI find a method in the index.** Then upload that pack and your data. You do not need to memorize 365 skill names

## A marketing medical center: Start with your problem, not a skill name

Think of a single-purpose AI Skill as a specialist clinic: it focuses on one kind of work. These **365 free marketing Skills are more like a marketing medical center**: a collection of methods from different disciplines, so you can start with the problem you are facing and find the right specialty.

For example, if an ad gets clicks but no orders, you may not yet know whether to examine the product's value, the ad message, or what happens on the website. You do not need to memorize Skill IDs. Describe the problem, let AI select one applicable method from the [method index](INDEX.md), and follow the [three-step guide](#start-your-first-task) to upload the method pack and relevant information. Work through one task at a time.

## Why I made these skills open source

I started “網路行銷零元本舖” to reduce information gaps and help businesses build their own marketing skills. Teaching was how I pursued that goal. With AI, I wanted to turn more than a decade of hands-on teaching and continuing client work into methods people could use alongside an AI assistant

One question kept coming up: if someone cannot afford an AI subscription, can they still put these methods to work? Opening up knowledge is only part of the task; people also need a practical way to use it

Another question matters just as much: if you are new to marketing, how do you know whether the AI's answer is right? These packs include the inputs, decision steps and checks, so you can work through a problem and inspect the result

That is what **Everyone** means here. Start with the free methods and practice data, then bring your own product, costs or draft. The goal is to build your ability to make a decision when a real problem comes up

The free edition provides usable marketing methods. Paid MCP services connect data and automate work; business implementations add custom development, guided adoption and ongoing maintenance. Start by using a method, then choose services around the work you actually need

## One box of cookies. How much do you actually keep?

Use the fictional [cookie costs](examples/cookie-costs.md) with [MK-059: contribution margin per item](packs/mk-059.md)

> How much contribution remains for each box of cookies after these costs? Show the formula and every cost item

| Input | Amount per box |
|---|---:|
| Net product revenue, after tax, discounts and refunds | NT$600 |
| Ingredients + packaging + additional production labor | NT$150 + 50 + 80 |
| Seller-paid shipping + platform and payment fees + allocated advertising | NT$60 + 30 + 40 |

**Your answer: NT$190 contribution per box — with every cost accounted for in this exercise**

```text
600 − (150 + 50 + 80 + 60 + 30 + 40) = 190
```

This fictional exercise has zero customer-paid shipping revenue and confirms that there are no other variable costs. Fixed salaries and rent are accounted for separately

Next, ask: “How should I encourage one-time customers to buy again?” The AI checks the same index, selects [MK-390: first repeat-purchase planning](packs/mk-390.md), and asks you to upload that pack and the customer data

## Start your first task

### 1. Get the English materials and upload the index

Use the `en/` directory in this repository, or select the English materials ZIP whose filename contains `-en-` from [Releases](https://github.com/backtrue/thinkwithblack-skill/releases)

After extracting the English ZIP, upload `INDEX.md` to an AI conversation that can read files. If using the full bilingual repository or materials ZIP, upload `en/INDEX.md`

### 2. Paste this prompt and describe your task

```text
Read the attached INDEX.md and choose a method for my current task
If you have not read the complete method, tell me which packs/*.md file to upload and wait for it before starting
When I change tasks, check the index again and tell me if you need another method pack

My task: [describe the work you want to do]
```

### 3. Upload the named pack and your data

Find the `.md` file the AI names inside the English `packs/` folder and upload it with your product details, report or draft. Each pack contains the full method, shared rules, required references and license

Try the [fictional practice data](examples/README.md) first, or add your completed [brand context template](context/MARKETING_CONTEXT.template.md) when you regularly work on the same brand

For a follow-up conversation, add the [case continuation summary](context/CASE_CONTEXT.template.md). The [three consulting tasks example](examples/consultant-decisions.md) shows method selection, recommendations and corrections

Upload the method itself as a file. You only need to paste the starter prompt once per conversation. See [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md) for follow-up questions and file-reading guidance

## Common questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need to restart the conversation for another task?</strong></summary>

Ask the next question. The starter prompt tells the AI to check the index again and ask for a different method pack if needed. For corrections or additional data on the same task, continue in the same conversation

In a new conversation, upload the index and the relevant pack again

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I share a Google Drive link instead of uploading a file?</strong></summary>

Share a readable link to the individual file and have the AI confirm that it read the complete method. If the link cannot be read, download that same `.md` and upload it. A folder link does not load every method into the conversation

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where are the method sources, and how can I contribute?</strong></summary>

Each pack preserves method sources and third-party licenses. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to suggest a correction

The public collection is defined by [free-content.json](free-content.json). See [RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md) for versions and maintenance, and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) for changes

</details>
