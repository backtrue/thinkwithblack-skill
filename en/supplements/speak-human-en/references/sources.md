## ThinkWithBlack additions

The local and distributed versions share the same English source. This synthesis adds an explicit edit / adaptation / drafting choice; a factual record before revision; a separate route for authorized factual updates; international-reader context for Taiwan cases; and a final check against the actual source

The worked examples were written for this skill. They test currency preservation, hypothetical product proposals, technical metric distinctions, author stance, and translated sentence structure

The upstream guides are editorial methods. This skill makes no empirical claim that a particular word identifies AI authorship or that these edits guarantee engagement or detector outcomes

## Maintenance

When revising a rule, check whether it improves reader understanding while preserving source meaning. Keep source revisions and licensing notices with redistributed versions. Update the examples when a rule changes so that the intended behavior remains inspectable
