# MK-052 | Checking pricing against the cost floor


## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A comparison of acceptable prices and profit constraints**

Without raw survey responses, produce only a questionnaire or analysis plan; do not fabricate answers

## Required inputs

Provide: PSM price candidates and sample conditions, net revenue and variable costs at each price, treatment of promotion and fulfillment costs, brand contribution target; fixed costs only for whole-business profit analysis

## Method

1. After deriving prices from PSM, put each price back into the contribution margin ratio calculation
2. If survey-acceptable prices cannot support a reasonable contribution margin ratio, reconsider production costs, product tier, or product design instead of forcing high costs onto the audience
3. The course material links contribution margin ratios to product roles: approximately 25% for traffic-driving/trial products, 40% for member/repeat-purchase products, and 25–35% for core products. These are course benchmarks; an actual brand must recalculate using real costs

## Calculations and interpretation

For each candidate price, contribution = comparable net revenue − variable costs; when net revenue is positive, contribution rate = contribution / net revenue. Count promotional/fulfillment costs once and show fixed costs separately. Compare with the brand target and PSM sample conditions; course percentages remain examples. Do not infer total profit without volume

## Verification cases

**MK-052-A1 | Candidate net revenue 100, variable cost 80, target 30%**

Expected: Contribution 20, rate 20%, ten percentage points below target; acceptance is not profit proof
