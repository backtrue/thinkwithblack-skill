# MK-099 | Backward scheduling for campaign preparation


## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Preparation of creative, pages, contact lists, and advertising**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. Set the campaign start date first, then work backward to schedule warm-up, pages, creative, survey/list collection, and existing-customer notifications
2. The course's classic structure is a 4-day warm-up, approximately 10 days of campaigning, and a 7-day post-campaign repeat-purchase period. It also recommends campaigns in 10-day units, lasting no more than 14 days
3. These durations are the course's reference structure. If the brand's campaign differs, preserve the phase tasks rather than forcing the dates

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
