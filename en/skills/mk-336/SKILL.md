# MK-336 | Article Differentiation Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-336.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Parts that repeat common claims or offer new judgments**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Check whether the article stops at basic definitions; if so, add common misconceptions, comparisons, beginner mistakes, and practical judgments
2. AI already has compilations and definitions; differentiation comes from experience, cases, evidence, and judgment
3. Distinctive content still needs source or experience support; do not disagree merely to appear unique

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
