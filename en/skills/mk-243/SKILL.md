# MK-243｜Compare Search Hypotheses with the Market

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-243.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Observed, unobserved, and contradictory evidence**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. Compare the first-stage demand_hypotheses with actual Search Terms
2. Use the statuses validated, partially_validated, not_observed, contradicted, and new_unexpected_demand
3. Store hypotheses separately from verified evidence; not observed does not mean nonexistent demand, so retain the period and visibility limitations of Search Terms
4. Produce Demand Map V2 so the next Ads/SEO round uses updated evidence

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
