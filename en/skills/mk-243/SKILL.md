# MK-243｜Compare Search Hypotheses with the Market

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-243.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Observed, unobserved, and contradictory evidence**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Raw inputs: original demand hypotheses, visible dated search terms, matching products/pages, customer questions, valid outcomes and report coverage

## Method Steps

1. Split each original hypothesis into question, recipient, situation and expected next step. Compare visible real Search Terms; keep Keywords and model suggestions separate
2. Mark support when the question, recipient and situation match; partial support identifies the supported condition and remaining gap. Shared word stems with different meanings do not validate a hypothesis
3. No matching visible term means not observed, with dates and report coverage retained. Mark contradiction only when real terms/customer records explicitly conflict with a condition. Record genuinely new questions separately
4. Supported hypotheses retain matched ad/page candidates; partial support narrows claims or adds landing content; unobserved ones need more evidence rather than exclusion; contradicted ones are revised/withdrawn. Check supply before proposing content or bounded tests for new demand. Semantic support alone does not justify increased spend
5. Deliver original hypothesis/term, supported scope, status and action. Update with newly visible terms, corrected situations/recipients or outcomes; retain inaccessible coverage and earlier versions

## Calculation and Interpretation

Assign states from original meaning and conditions, without invented validation scores

Hypothetical check: a personal-use raincoat hypothesis and a raincoat-as-gift query support only the product topic, so mark partial support. A later matched customer statement confirms personal commuting use and supports that situation
