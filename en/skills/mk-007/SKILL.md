# MK-007 | Turning features into customer benefits

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-007.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A feature, advantage, and benefit translation table**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. Use FABE to turn a Feature into an Advantage, then into a Benefit the Persona actually cares about
2. A Benefit must describe a specific gain or change for the role, not simply restate the feature with synonyms
3. Add Evidence last to build trust; without evidence, do not present the Benefit as a verified effect

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-007-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-007-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
