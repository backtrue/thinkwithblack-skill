# MK-317｜Page Title Check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-317.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Alignment between the topic, demand, and title**

Permission in robots or the existence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine site technical status and content quality into a single conclusion

## Required Inputs

Page URL, actual title/body, page job, audience and search-question evidence

## Method Steps

1. Read the page's <title> and confirm that it explains what the page is and includes the core topic target users actually search for
2. The title sets the tone in the page head and must match actual content, rather than contain only a brand slogan
3. Analyze target users and search questions before consolidating and rewriting; do not sacrifice readability to stuff in words

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
