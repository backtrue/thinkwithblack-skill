# MK-012 | One-sentence brand positioning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-012.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **One sentence stating who it serves, what it solves, and how it differs**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. At minimum, one-sentence positioning should let an outside reader understand who the brand is, what it does, whom it serves, and which topic it relates to
2. Go beyond an abstract slogan; include an identifiable category/service and its intended audience
3. Use the sentence as the core of consistency across platforms, adapting its length to each platform without changing the positioning

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-012-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-012-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
