# MK-342 | Standard Brand Name Organization

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-342.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A primary name, aliases, and wording that avoids confusion**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Inventory the brand names used on the official website, Google Business Profile, FB, IG, LinkedIn, YouTube, and other official channels
2. Find inconsistent names, confusing abbreviations, and incorrect destinations; establish one standard name
3. Standardization means making official channels consistently identify the same brand, rather than opening more accounts

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
