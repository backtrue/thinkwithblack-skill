# MK-329 | Step Module Fit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-329.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Steps with order, prerequisites, and completion conditions**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Use a step module when answering “How do I do this / What is the process?”
2. Retain the action and necessary conditions in every step so AI can directly extract the process
3. Do not force content without a sequence into steps

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
