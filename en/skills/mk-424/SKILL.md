# MK-424 | Successful Creative Reuse Topic Selection

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-424.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **New uses for previously effective content**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. A previously successful post may be reposted monthly as part of the social content schedule
2. Before selecting it, confirm that it still fits current products, campaigns, and audiences; expired offers or seasonal contexts cannot be reused unchanged
3. The original course materials provide no universal numerical threshold for “best-selling”; select candidates using the brand's existing performance data rather than inventing a fixed engagement rate

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
