# MK-161｜Meta Ad Hierarchy Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-161.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Roles of campaigns, ad sets, and ads**

Use actual tools and current official specifications to establish platform options and placement availability; old course screenshots do not establish current capabilities

## Required Inputs

Provide: account configuration snapshots, business goals, creative, events, and authorization information

## Method Steps

1. Plan with three levels: the campaign sets ad type/budget strategy; the ad set sets optimization goal, budget, audience, and placements; the ad sets creative and copy
2. In most cases, place the budget at ad set level for later optimization; consider campaign-level budget when testing many audiences and letting the system allocate it
3. Keep the three levels distinct so later analysis can identify the level at which a problem occurs

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify current platform settings and available audiences; this catalog does not establish an existing account read/write integration
