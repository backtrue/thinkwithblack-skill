# MK-161｜Meta Ad Hierarchy Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-161.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Roles of campaigns, ad sets, and ads**

Use actual tools and current official specifications to establish platform options and placement availability; old course screenshots do not establish current capabilities

## Required Inputs

Raw inputs: campaign/ad-set setting snapshots, business tasks, differences to control between tests, total budget and per-group spend requirements, creatives/events and available settings

## Method Steps

1. Record actual campaign, ad-set and ad settings and tasks. Count a budget once at its configured level. Retain a workable existing structure rather than move budgets based on labels
2. When audiences need separate evidence, a business requires protected group spend, or groups have different tasks/constraints, select a setup that preserves group control. Verify what the current settings actually control; one campaign budget is not a separate allocation for every group
3. When groups serve the same result, unequal allocation is permitted and no group must spend a specified amount, consider aggregate allocation. Verify objectives, events, periods and available controls. Judge overall results; unequal delivery is not a fair creative test
4. If required allocations exceed the affordable total, reduce the tested groups, defer some tests or present feasible options. Do not increase the total automatically. Without final allocation requirements, deliver conditional layer choices and exact settings to verify
5. Deliver each layer task, budget owner, group controls and evaluation scope. Rechoose when tasks/protected spend change, groups lack needed evidence or actual settings cannot provide control. Diagnose creative/audience issues at their respective layer

## Calculation and Interpretation

Required controlled spend is the sum of necessary group allocations, compared with the same-period total. Do not add another budget per ad

Hypothetical check: a total of 1,000 and two controlled 500 tests support a controllable group setup. If protected group spend is later removed and only the same overall result matters, aggregate allocation becomes eligible. Two required 800 tests call for reducing scope, not raising the total to 1,600
