# MK-112 | Coupon-use condition design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-112.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Eligible audience, scope, and validity period**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Use coupons in survey-based warm-up campaigns or first-purchase incentives to give survey completers a reason to buy afterward
2. Coupon conditions must serve a clear audience and campaign purpose; do not turn member-only benefits or warm-up incentives into public ongoing discounts
3. The original course material supplies no fixed discount percentage, minimum spend, or expiry formula; derive conditions from gross margin and campaign design

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
