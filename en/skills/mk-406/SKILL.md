# MK-406 | Seven-Day LINE Flow for Non-Buyers

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-406.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A draft with conditions, messages, and exit rules**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Design an initial 7-day message flow for newly joined non-buyers; this is the course materials' minimum post-course action plan
2. Start with the joining incentive and welcome message, then use tags / behavior to provide education, trust, and reasons for a first purchase
3. The course materials do not require messages every day for seven days; design frequency and content around the brand's actual strategy, and do not misstate 7 days as 7 messages

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
