# MK-036 | Interpreting four types of search intent

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-036.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Intent classifications, supporting reasons, and uncertain items**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: The product or service, customers' own words, existing pages, and actions the company can support

## Method

1. Classify the literal search wording as informational, navigational, commercial investigation, or transactional
2. Informational searches seek understanding; navigational searches seek a specific destination; commercial investigation compares options; transactional searches indicate readiness to act
3. When literal wording is insufficient, mark "undetermined" and list the missing clues. These categories are not fields in the Google Ads interface

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need
