# MK-345 | Brand Category and Service Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-345.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Problems caused by descriptions in the wrong category**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Verify how each official / external presence describes the brand's service category, core products / services, and audience
2. Flag positioning conflicts when some platforms say premium and others say budget, or when a strategy service is described as a tool
3. Use the actual business approved by the brand; do not expand into nonexistent categories for SEO / GEO

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
