# MK-125｜Everyday Role Section

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-125.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Copy showing how the product fits into users' lives**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. Beyond solving pain points and describing a desirable future, explain the role the product plays in the Persona's life
2. Translate features into everyday value through Who / When / Why Me; speed alone is not a benefit, but being faster than alternatives in a particular situation is
3. Support the role with a real usage context; do not substitute an abstract brand slogan for an everyday role

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
