# MK-122 | Ideal scenarios for a sales page

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-122.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Post-use scenarios without fabricated experiences**

A content review cannot replace user-behavior data; without performance data, do not assert why conversions declined

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, Persona, selling points, real evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method

1. For Personas unsuited to fear-based pain messaging, build section 2 around an ideal future
2. Ask about positive moments, where they happen, time/money saved, improved relationships, and whom the person wants to share them with
3. Ground the ideal scenario in actual product features; do not write generic inspiration unrelated to the product

## Calculations and interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials need original support; produce only a page draft and do not publish automatically
