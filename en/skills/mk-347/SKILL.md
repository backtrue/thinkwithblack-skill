# MK-347 | Brand Entity and Founder Relationship Mapping

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-347.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Actual relationships among people, organizations, and services**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. For service businesses / professional brands, document public relationships among founders, physicians, consultants, teams, brands, and service pages
2. Connect people, the brand, and services to provide identity and business verification signals
3. Use only actual relationships that are public and approved by the brand; do not infer titles, qualifications, or affiliations

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
