# MK-114 | Matching campaign messages to customer groups

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-114.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Main messages for different customer groups in the same campaign**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Combine the month's essential demand, Persona pain points/desires, and product USPs into the campaign message
2. New-customer communication answers "Why make my first purchase this month?"; member-benefit bundles for existing customers answer "Why buy again or stock up this time?"
3. Choose different features of the same product for different Personas; do not send every product feature to every customer group at once

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
