# MK-128｜Brand Rationale Section

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-128.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Supported brand beliefs and commitments**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. In the ninth cell, reintroduce who the brand is, why it made the product, and the real commitments it is willing to make
2. This cell helps potential customers who reach the end remember the brand and its WHY, building a connection between person and brand
3. Do not add founder stories, missions, or commitments that the brand has not supplied

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
