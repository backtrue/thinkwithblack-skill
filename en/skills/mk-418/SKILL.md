# MK-418 | Weekly Social Content Schedule

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-418.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Content roles for education, testimonials, campaigns, and other tasks**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Organize weekly content around the month's marketing theme
2. The course materials' baseline cadence is one educational resource, one KOL / KOC roundup or comparison, one monthly-theme piece, and one repost of a previously successful post per week
3. This is a suggested course cadence, not a mandatory volume for every brand; when production resources are insufficient, retain the content roles before adjusting counts

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
