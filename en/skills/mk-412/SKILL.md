# MK-412 | Newsletter Preview Text Draft

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-412.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Preview summaries that continue the subject line**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Preview text should continue the subject line rather than repeat it
2. The course materials use approximately 16 Chinese characters on mobile as a concise design basis
3. Together, the subject and preview should communicate content and benefits; do not include nonexistent offers or deadlines

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
