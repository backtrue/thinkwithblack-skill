# MK-162｜Meta Naming Convention Generation

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-162.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A configurable rather than hard-coded naming table**

Use actual tools and current official specifications to establish platform options and placement availability; old course screenshots do not establish current capabilities

## Required Inputs

Provide: account configuration snapshots, business goals, creative, events, and authorization information

## Method Steps

1. Campaign names include objective, advertised product, evergreen/campaign, and new/existing customer, for example “Traffic｜Evergreen｜Milk foam｜New customers”
2. Distinguish ad sets by audience, demographics, and placements, for example “Delivery workers｜25-35｜IG Reels+Stories”
3. Ad names can follow the messaging categories of the three-line copy; the goal is quick future identification and analysis

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify current platform settings and available audiences; this catalog does not establish an existing account read/write integration
