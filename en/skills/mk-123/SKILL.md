# MK-123｜Three-Solution Section

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-123.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A mapping of pain points to three USPs**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. In the third cell, introduce the product as the solution; in cells four–six, select only the three features the Persona cares about most
2. Each feature must connect to a pain-point solution, a desirable future, or the role the product plays in everyday life
3. Select differentiators such as something others lack, a better version of what others offer, more than competitors at a similar quality level, or a lower price for a comparable quantity; each must have a factual product basis

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
