# MK-061 | Break-even revenue calculation

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-061.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A break-even table based on fixed expenses and contribution margin ratio**

Recalculability does not mean the data is complete; break-even points and cost ceilings apply only under the stated assumptions

## Required inputs

Provide: Fixed costs and contribution rate for the same period, or net revenue and variable costs needed to derive that rate

For method questions, provide the formula, variables and an example directly; for a calculation, ask only for missing operands

## Method

1. Break-even revenue = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin ratio
2. Assuming a contribution margin ratio of approximately 30–40%, the course material offers roughly three times fixed costs as a quick estimate; use the actual contribution margin ratio for a formal calculation
3. When the contribution margin ratio is too low, reduce costs/adjust prices first instead of forcing traffic growth at an extremely low contribution ratio

## Calculations and interpretation

Break-even revenue = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin ratio

Retain original values, units, periods, and substituted formulas. The formula is not an unconditional guarantee of results

Define gross profit, contribution margin, cost denominators, and periods explicitly; do not copy classroom example numbers as universal thresholds
