# MK-186｜Remarketing Creative Matching

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-186.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Content matched to each funnel stage**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. Viewed but did not add to cart: prioritize urgency/scarcity or third-party testimonial/review creative
2. Added to cart but did not buy: prioritize urgency reminders or cost-barrier creative such as new-customer credit/starter bundles
3. Re-engage Page/IG engagers with trust and reasons to buy; do not repeat the same acquisition creative at every stage

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
