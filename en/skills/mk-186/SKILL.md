# MK-186｜Remarketing Creative Matching

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-186.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Content matched to each funnel stage**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Raw inputs: matched events/orders, customer-service or nonbuyer statements, product and landing pages, checkout charges/errors, creative rights, actual offers and audience exclusions

## Method Steps

1. Fix audience, product, event definitions, period and deduplication. Separate purchasers, unsubscribed people and ineligible offer recipients. Totals for different events are not one cohort dropping off; use matched browsing/checkout records for cause checks
2. Match evidence to a response: explicit trust concerns plus a relevant usable testimonial support trust creative; questions about use, specifications or eligibility support information; an explicit cost concern confirmed in that checkout supports a cost explanation or actual eligible offer
3. Payment errors, missing pages, unavailable delivery or unusable offers require a process/landing fix first. Discounts or urgency creative do not resolve an unexamined malfunction
4. Without cause records, retain trust, information and cost hypotheses and select one that a next customer-service response or bounded creative test can distinguish. Use reminders only for actual deadlines/stock limits. Explain known charges directly rather than ask customers to diagnose their barrier
5. For each match give the source audience, observed evidence, eligibility, creative promise, page location and action. New causes, expired offers or updated purchase states replace the original match. Multiple supported causes remain separate

## Calculation and Interpretation

The funnel stage selects records to inspect; cause evidence selects creative and action. Update using matched valid purchases and costs, rather than infer that better clicks resolved a barrier

Hypothetical check: a customer says shipping is unexpected and the matched checkout confirms it, supporting an actual cost explanation. A later record shows payment repeatedly failing; change to fixing payment and withdraw the offer-only response
