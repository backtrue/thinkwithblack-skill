# MK-103 | Countdown-phase communication planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-103.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Reminders based on real closing conditions**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goal, actual deadline, products/offers, evidence of quantity or stock limits, available prior high-intent audience and contact eligibility

## Method

1. Use countdown creative in the final three days; the course recommends updating countdown messages daily
2. Concentrate audiences with high-intent events such as VC and ATC accumulated earlier in the campaign into purchase-conversion efforts
3. Limited-time/limited-quantity messages must match real campaign deadlines and inventory; do not manufacture urgency

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
