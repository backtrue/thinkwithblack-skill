# MK-416 | Unsubscribe and Inactivity Suppression Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-416.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Recipients and conditions for stopping messages**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Member communication should be segmented; the course materials explicitly say not to continue indiscriminate eDM sends to people who do not open them
2. Use current lists each month; reduce frequency for low-potential and dormant members according to campaign objectives, with course examples sending SMS only on major occasions
3. The original course materials do not provide a complete legal unsubscribe process or automated suppression algorithm; respect existing platform unsubscribe / block statuses during execution, without pretending the materials define technical rules

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
