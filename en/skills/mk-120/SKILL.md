# MK-120 | One-sentence sales-page positioning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-120.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A clear answer to who the product is for and what it solves**

A content review cannot replace user-behavior data; without performance data, do not assert why conversions declined

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, Persona, selling points, real evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method

1. Use one-sentence positioning in the first section of the nine-section framework, speaking first to the situation and value proposition the Persona cares about
2. The product may or may not appear; the point is to make viewers feel the page speaks to their own situation
3. Find positioning through scenario elements such as When, Where, What, Who, and Why/Why Me; prioritize value over price

## Calculations and interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials need original support; produce only a page draft and do not publish automatically
