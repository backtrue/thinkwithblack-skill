# MK-079 | Trial-product design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-079.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Specification proposals that lower the barrier to a first trial**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: SKUs, product costs, customer groups, purchase records, and product lines

## Method

1. Trial products/first-purchase bundles lower the psychological barrier to buying for the first time through small sizes, included shipping, or trial combinations
2. Trial products should identify audiences suitable for an ongoing relationship, rather than attracting only one-time bargain seekers
3. Design a repeat-purchase sequence after the trial; the course example invites the first repeat purchase after 10–14 days

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
