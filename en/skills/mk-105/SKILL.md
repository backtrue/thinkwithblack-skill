# MK-105 | Reusable campaign-page planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-105.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Pages to reuse annually and items to update**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Existing campaign page and URL, recurring campaign/event history, current theme, products, conditions and assets, tracking and links to preserve

## Method

1. Use the same campaign-page URL for monthly/annual recurring events so external content from different years points to the same page
2. Update the theme, products, and creative each year while keeping the URL where possible to build search and external-link assets
3. Product pages handle ongoing introductions; recurring campaign pages handle time-limited promotions and remarketing traffic. Keep their page tasks distinct

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
