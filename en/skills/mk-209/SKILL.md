# MK-209｜Diagnose High-Engagement, Low-Sales Posts

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-209.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Gaps between engagement and the business task**

Past success supports candidates and hypotheses only; shared content elements are not proven causes of success

## Required Inputs

Provide: Post IDs, cross-audience performance, creative, and seed-list quality

## Method Steps

1. High engagement with low sales does not directly qualify a post as a Hero Post
2. Check whether CTR is high, whether ATC/Purchase follows, and whether the page experience and audience task align
3. If it generates engagement without business outcomes, retain it as an engagement/education asset when appropriate, but do not treat it as the main conversion asset

## Calculation and Interpretation

Aggregate by Post ID and distinguish advertising contexts; high engagement or high ROAS alone does not guarantee scalability
