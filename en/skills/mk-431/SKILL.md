# MK-431 | New and Returning Customer Traffic Comparison

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-431.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Comparisons with consistent periods and new / returning customer definitions**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Provide: GA4, GSC, advertising, and sales exports, metric definitions, and costs

## Method steps

1. Compare new- and returning-customer traffic separately; do not blend their conversion rates, average order values, and acquisition costs into one overall average
2. New-customer sources may include organic / referral / cpc / manual UTM; returning customers often include direct, email, LINE, SMS, and other return-visit sources
3. Base source classification on actual UTM and GA data; the course materials give typical illustrative settings, rather than a single mandatory standard

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify data sources, dates, currencies, denominators, and attribution; sums across platforms do not equal unique reach or incremental impact
