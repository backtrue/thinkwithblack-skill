# MK-392 | New-Product Priority for High-Value Members

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-392.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Eligible members and a new-product communication plan**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. Give loyal members priority for new products / special initiatives that sustain a sense of recognition
2. Examples in the course materials include VIP-only new-product prices, priority access to closed-door store events, and priority participation in special initiatives
3. High-potential members can receive early trials of classic products; do not give completely identical benefits to different member tiers

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
