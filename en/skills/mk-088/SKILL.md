# MK-088 | Channel-exclusive bundle design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-088.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Product-bundle differences across channels**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: Transactions and audience by channel, fees and revenue shares, product bundles, willingness-to-pay or choice evidence, stock and fulfillment conditions

## Method

1. Different channels have different audiences, selling costs, and willingness to pay, so channel-exclusive bundles can avoid direct price comparison
2. Place itself can raise or lower willingness to pay; use different bundles for the brand website, platforms, and department stores/physical channels
3. Bundle differences must reflect genuine product/service differences; do not invent specifications to create false distinctions

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
