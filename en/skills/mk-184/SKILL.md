# MK-184｜Genuine Deadline Reminder Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-184.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Reminders based only on valid offers and deadlines**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. Urgency-based remarketing is for people who may already be inclined to buy but have not yet ordered
2. The course emphasizes that a hard deadline is not always required; genuine limited quantity can also work, with a reason to buy immediately as the core
3. Do not create fake countdowns, stock levels, or deadlines

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
