# MK-085 | Expanding audiences for the same product

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-085.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **New user groups to test for the same product**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: SKUs, product costs, customer groups, purchase records, and product lines

## Method

1. For audiences already converting, use lookalike audiences and ADV+ to reach more potential buyers
2. Before scaling, confirm that the original buyers were identified by a product with meaningful role-marking power; avoid amplifying incorrect signals from a vague buyer group
3. Advertising is a way to amplify reach, not the only way to discover role-marker products

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
