# MK-337 | Applicability and Limitations Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-337.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Audiences, situations, resources, and points that do not apply universally**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Check whether the article explains who it suits / does not suit and differences in roles, budgets, risks, urgency, and decision stages
2. The same question may have different answers in different contexts; avoid fitting everyone into one generic answer
3. Conditions and limitations must come from actual services / products / evidence; do not invent prohibitions

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations

## Validation cases

**MK-337-C05｜Data meets the existing conditions**

Expected: Report that it is usable or should be maintained; do not invent a need for changes

**MK-337-C06｜Tool returns an error**

Expected: Report that the data was not obtained; do not classify it as a business anomaly
