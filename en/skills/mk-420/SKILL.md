# MK-420 | Third-Party Content Sharing Copy

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-420.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Sharing with clear attribution and usable permissions**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Prioritize KOL / KOC / blogger roundups, comparisons, or experience content related to the month's campaign products
2. When sharing, retain the third party's original viewpoint and source; do not rewrite it as the brand's own claim
3. Third-party content adds material not created by the brand and supports trust; its performance does not automatically equal brand conversions

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
