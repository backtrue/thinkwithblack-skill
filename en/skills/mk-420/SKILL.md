# MK-420 | Third-Party Content Sharing Copy

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-420.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Sharing with clear attribution and usable permissions**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Original third-party article/readable content, author/source, sharing purpose/audience, applicable quotation/adaptation permissions and relevant campaign facts

## Method steps

1. Read the source and separate author opinion, verifiable facts and the brand’s additions
2. Check the sharing form/permitted scope, retaining attribution/link without recasting it as the brand’s experience
3. Write the complete post using an original summary and necessary brief quotes, checking meaning, conditions and links

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
