# MK-089 | Three-sided product-pricing check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-089.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Conflicts among cost, market, and willingness to pay**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: SKU costs and target contribution, comparable market prices and channel conditions, audience willingness-to-pay or choice evidence, adjustable offers

## Method

1. Check three sides of pricing together: market prices/channels, the value the audience will pay for, and costs/contribution margin ratio
2. Starting from costs, calculate fixed/variable costs and break-even first, then find a market audience that accepts that value. No one is obliged to pay for the brand's high costs
3. Starting from the market, if the audience's willingness to pay is below the current cost structure, revisit cost/value allocation or change the product tier

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
