# MK-330 | Comparison Table Module Fit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-330.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Proposed tables with comparison dimensions and information gaps**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Use a comparison or decision table when answering “What is different / How do I choose?”
2. Comparison fields should support a decision, such as who an option suits or does not suit, and differences in price / risk / conditions
3. Tables make answers extractable; do not add irrelevant fields for appearance alone

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
