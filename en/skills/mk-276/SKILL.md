# MK-276｜Demand Gen Audience Diagnosis

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-276.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Performance differences between audience groups**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Views or clicks do not automatically stand in for sales; do not directly rank channels measured on different bases

## Required Inputs

Results by audience, mature outcomes, period/attribution definitions, creative, channel, destination and goal

## Method Steps

1. Organize comparable data by audience; inspect impression/spend opportunity first
2. List differences in creative, channel, destination, bidding and timing as potential confounds
3. Calculate comparable outcomes/costs against brand targets; distinguish supported observations from causal hypotheses and specify the next comparison

## Calculation and Interpretation

Cost per result = matched spend / valid results; zero denominator is undefined. Keep click/view outcomes distinct from sales; insufficient opportunity remains pending

## Verification Cases

**MK-276-A1 | One group has no impressions**

Expected: No comparison opportunity, not a loser

**MK-276-A2 | Both audience and creative change**

Expected: Identify confounding, not a single cause
