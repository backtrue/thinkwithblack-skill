# MK-038 | Designing a reasonable next step

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-038.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Actions matched to the maturity of the need**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: The product or service, customers' own words, existing pages, and actions the company can support

## Method

1. The next step should continue the current search intent: informational users first need understanding, comparison users need differences, and transactional users may be ready to buy/book directly
2. Check whether the action jumps too far ahead or keeps ready customers going in circles
3. The next step must be an action the company can actually provide

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need
