# MK-331 | FAQ Module Fit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-331.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **FAQ sections supported by actual question demand**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. FAQs can address common customer service questions, common search questions, or questions in the article needing a separate answer
2. Phrase each item as a real question, give a direct answer first, then add necessary context
3. FAQs are not for keyword stuffing; every question needs a source showing actual demand

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
