# MK-361 | AI Answer Record Table

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-361.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Platforms, dates, questions, complete answers, and sources**

One answer does not establish a stable ranking; do not simulate current results when actual answers have not been obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: actual AI answers, queries, dates, platforms, and cited URLs

## Method steps

1. Record the date, platform, test question, question type, and observed AI answer each time
2. Also record citations, mentions, description accuracy, neighboring brands, preliminary interpretation, and next steps
3. Keep tabular records comparable across tests, rather than screenshots alone

## Calculation and interpretation

Retain questions, dates, platforms, and actual answers; one result does not represent answers shown to all users
