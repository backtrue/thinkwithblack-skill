# MK-413 | Newsletter Body and CTA

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-413.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Content, benefits, buttons, and destination pages**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Use the subject and preview text to create a reason to open the newsletter, then highlight the key points and guide clicks in the body
2. Retain only benefits and information needed for the current campaign or member task, with a clear CTA
3. Send member messages by segment; use different content for different member statuses instead of indiscriminate mass messages
4. Content and CTAs must lead to an available page or action; do not describe a draft as already sent

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
