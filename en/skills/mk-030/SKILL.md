# MK-030 | Role-marker counterexample check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-030.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Alternative explanations such as gifting and purchasing for others**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Check whether the product actually rules out other purchase reasons. If it may be a gift, a purchase for someone else, or serve multiple situations, its marking strength is insufficient
2. Function-specific products can define a role more directly; contextual products need support from surveys, email-marketing tags, or remarketing paths
3. Do not assign every buyer to a role merely because the product name resembles something used by that group

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior

## Verification cases

**MK-030-C05 | An order contains maternity and baby products**

Expected: Do not immediately identify the buyer as a mother; separate the buying and using roles

**MK-030-C06 | Different clues support two situations**

Expected: Keep both candidates and request the data that would distinguish them
