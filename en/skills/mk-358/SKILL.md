# MK-358 | AI Search Test Question Bank

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-358.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Definition, comparison, context, and decision questions**

One answer does not establish a stable ranking; do not simulate current results when actual answers have not been obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: Approved brand/product/service facts, customer decisions and question sources, test market and purpose; existing questions help deduplication, while AI answers and citations are recorded after testing

## Method steps

1. Build an AI search question bank covering at least definitions, comparisons, methods, contexts, recommendations, and decisions
2. Questions should simulate real user needs, rather than testing only the brand name
3. The course assignment starts with a 30-question bank, then selects the most important 10 for live testing

## Calculation and interpretation

Retain questions, dates, platforms, and actual answers; one result does not represent answers shown to all users
