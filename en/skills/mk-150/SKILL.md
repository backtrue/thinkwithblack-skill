# MK-150｜Match Reviews to Purchase Barriers

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-150.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Which review can answer which concern**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Provide: actual reviews, customer service records, collaboration content, permissions, and performance data

## Method Steps

1. Map review content to purchase barriers to test, such as distrust of the brand, doubts about product results, or whether anyone has actually used it
2. For viewers who did not add to cart, prioritize reviews/third-party testimonials to test a trust gap; if costs are the main barrier after adding to cart, reviews may not be the first solution
3. Use a review only for the concern it actually supports; do not treat all reviews as universal remarketing creative

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement
