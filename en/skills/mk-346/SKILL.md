# MK-346 | Official Brand Link Comparison

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-346.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Cross-platform connections and incorrect links**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Check whether official channels link to the correct website / brand page, rather than incorrect, outdated, or unrelated pages
2. Links among About pages, social accounts, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, YouTube, and other channels should confirm the same brand identity
3. An existing link does not establish content accuracy; verify names and descriptions too

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
