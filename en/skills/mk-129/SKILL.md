# MK-129｜Sales Page Section Order Diagnosis

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-129.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Problems in readers' understanding and decision sequence**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Page text or complete sections to diagnose, target Persona, intended page job, product claims and available evidence

## Method Steps

1. Check the nine-cell sequence: one-sentence situational positioning → pain point/ideal situation → product solution → three core features → third-party testimonials → promotion/bundle → brand WHY
2. If the page opens with product specifications and no Persona situation, fix the opening first; if trust is missing, prioritize the seventh cell; if the reason to buy is missing, inspect the eighth cell and CTA
3. Diagnose which layer is missing; the presence of a nine-cell framework does not justify filling every cell with irrelevant content

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
