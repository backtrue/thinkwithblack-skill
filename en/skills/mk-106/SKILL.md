# MK-106 | First-purchase offer design for new customers

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-106.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Purchase reasons and product bundles for new customers**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. New-customer promotional bundles lower the first-purchase barrier. Common approaches include buy-one-get-multiple offers, multi-product bundles, and small-portion/small-volume trial sets
2. Prioritize sales-support products, bundles, and trial prices to increase perceived value, avoiding deep direct discounts on regular products that create price-comparison and gross-margin problems
3. Check new-customer promotional bundles against the contribution margin table; the course recommends a target contribution margin ratio of at least 25%

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
