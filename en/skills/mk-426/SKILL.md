# MK-426 | Social Content Performance Review

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-426.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Separate assessments of engagement, clicks, and business tasks**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Review content exposure / engagement separately from downstream business tasks; do not equate high engagement with high revenue
2. Review what each monthly content role accomplished: educational resources, third-party roundups and comparisons, marketing themes, and reused posts
3. Keep reusable high-performing posts as next month's candidates, after checking whether their context and campaign still apply
4. The original course materials provide no universal social performance formula; use metrics the brand can actually obtain, without inventing fixed thresholds

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
