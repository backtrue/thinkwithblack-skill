# MK-013 | Competitive advantage comparison

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-013.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A comparison of brand differences within the evidence available**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. A USP should first help the role compare the product with what they currently use, their habitual solution, or other options they have considered
2. Describe only genuine product Advantages over alternatives; do not invent competitors' weaknesses
3. Without competitor/alternative information, list items to compare. Do not state "better than ordinary products" as a fact

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-013-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-013-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
