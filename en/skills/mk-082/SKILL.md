# MK-082 | Planning knowledge-based entry points

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-082.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **An entry point that answers questions before introducing products**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: Original audience questions, observed SERP or service evidence, existing pages and supported products, candidate partners/traffic sources and their evidence

## Method

1. When the product line is insufficient, use knowledge products to qualify audiences first, then convert through remarketing ads
2. Knowledge products can include curated comparisons, Q&A articles, and event press releases. Choose sources and partners using actual SERP, customer-service, or similar data
3. Knowledge products aim to build a browsing audience with identifiable needs and connect it to relevant products, not merely acquire traffic

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
