# MK-341 | Before-and-After Article Revision Comparison

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-341.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Changes, retained facts, and items still lacking evidence**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Before revising, save the first analysis, the weakest 3 checkpoints, and the original passages
2. Revise the gaps, run a second analysis, and save the results after revision
3. Deliver a before-and-after comparison and one sentence explaining why AI citation was previously difficult; do not report only an improved score

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
