# MK-098 | Annual new-product sequencing

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-098.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Progression among new products, bestsellers, and campaigns**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

New products and current bestsellers, launch conditions/dates, product and collaboration-packaging costs, production/sourcing timeline, campaign goal, stock and budget

## Method

1. Prioritize new-product launch dates that align with relevant industry holidays, recurring exhibitions, or the brand anniversary
2. A new product need not be 100% new; it may be an IP collaboration, seasonal packaging, or a new combination of existing products
3. Include production/purchasing costs for entirely new products and collaboration-packaging costs separately in the contribution margin assessment

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws

## Verification cases

**MK-098-A1 | New product sourcing completes after the planned campaign**

Expected: Flag the dependency conflict and revise the handoff date or retain the bestseller; do not call the new product launch-ready
