# MK-044 | Search-hypothesis validation checklist

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-044.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Interview, search-term, or page evidence needed for each hypothesis**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: The product or service, customers' own words, existing pages, and actions the company can support

## Method

1. List the hypotheses to validate for each need path: event, search phrase, intent, ad answer, page, and next step
2. Prioritize verification through interviews, search terms, customer-service records, and existing pages
3. Ask four self-check questions: Is the event specific? Does the search phrase sound natural? Do the ad, page, and action address the same question? Which part still lacks evidence?

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need

## Verification cases

**MK-044-C05 | An ad promotes a service that the page does not explain**

Expected: Record a verifiable promise gap; do not immediately conclude it caused all abandonment

Expected: Produce only a need map awaiting validation, not a claimed finding from actual research
