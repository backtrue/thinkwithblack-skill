# MK-397 | Invitations to Members Willing to Recommend

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-397.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Authorized invitations to share and provide feedback**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: Recommendation question and scale, actual scores and dates, Promoter definition, deidentified member key, campaign and contact eligibility

## Method steps

1. Member check-in emails ask only the NPS / likelihood-to-recommend question to identify Promoters
2. Promoters can form a base list for member event invitations
3. Before inviting them, verify contact permission and eligibility for the current event; willingness to recommend is not consent to participate

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages

## Verification cases

Expected: Do not label Promoter; request question, scale and response
