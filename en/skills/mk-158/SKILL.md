# MK-158｜Trust Gap Creative List

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-158.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Locations needing reviews, evidence, or explanations**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Actual pages and claim locations; target audience and known concerns; existing testimonials, reviews, and professional evidence with sources and permissions; contacts who can supply missing material

## Method Steps

1. Prioritize authentic user testimonials, third-party KOL/KOC/bloggers, professional authorities, and media/other verifiable endorsements in the trust-gap asset inventory
2. Where the industry especially depends on professional trust, the course recommends retaining collaboration with professional authorities
3. If assets are missing, list the gaps and obtainable sources first; do not generate fake social proof or celebrity endorsements
4. Map each concern to a claim and page location, check what the existing evidence actually supports, and list missing explanations or assets, obtainable sources, owners, and priority rationale

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement
