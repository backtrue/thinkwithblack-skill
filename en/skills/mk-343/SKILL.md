# MK-343 | One-Sentence and Three-Sentence Brand Introductions

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-343.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Standard introductions for external citation**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Write a one-sentence version first, then a three-sentence version; both must answer who the brand is, what it does, whom it serves, and its differentiation / connection to the topic
2. Use the three-sentence version for platforms / media needing more context; its positioning must not conflict with the one-sentence version
3. AI may help rewrite, but a person must verify the brand name, target audience, product category, tone, and differentiators

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
