# MK-116 | Campaign contact-list building design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-116.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Participation and subscription paths that clearly disclose their purpose**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Ideally, warm-up campaigns record Purchases or first-party contacts; secondary options are ATC, VC, or LINE OA contacts
2. Survey-based warm-up collects first-party data and willingness to pay; reservation-based warm-up suits physical stores/events; preorder vouchers can also record purchase events
3. Plan to turn collected contacts into remarketing audiences during the main campaign; do not collect a list without planning its subsequent use

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
