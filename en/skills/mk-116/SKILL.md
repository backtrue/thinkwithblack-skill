# MK-116 | Campaign contact-list building design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-116.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Participation and subscription paths that clearly disclose their purpose**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Campaign goal, product/conditions, current form or joining flow, collected fields, purpose/disclosure, follow-up channels and contact eligibility

## Method

1. State which data the campaign collects and whether it supports participation or specified future contact; check existing campaign rules and disclosures
2. Define valid participation/subscription and eligibility from the actual flow; preserve an explicit choice and non-opt-in path where joining requires that choice
3. Map required fields, collection timing, purpose and contact eligibility; mark lists with missing purpose/eligibility evidence as pending
4. Deliver form/joining copy, follow-up messages and flow checks; a view or click alone is not subscription consent

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
