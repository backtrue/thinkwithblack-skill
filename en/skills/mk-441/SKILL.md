# MK-441 | GSC Search Opportunity Selection

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-441.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Candidates based on impressions, clicks, rankings, and demand**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Provide: GA4, GSC, advertising, and sales exports, metric definitions, and costs

## Method steps

1. Use GSC to observe search impressions and traffic, with sustained search-impression growth as the long-term goal
2. Select GSC queries for which more impressions are desired; the course materials prioritize high-CTR keywords with positions greater than 10 for Google Ads testing
3. Feed converting paid-search terms back into SEO / blogger content, using paid search as a testing ground for organic search

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify data sources, dates, currencies, denominators, and attribution; sums across platforms do not equal unique reach or incremental impact
