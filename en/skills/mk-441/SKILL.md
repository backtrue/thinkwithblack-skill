# MK-441 | GSC Search Opportunity Selection

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-441.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Candidates based on impressions, clicks, rankings, and demand**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Raw inputs: GSC query/page/date/device/region impressions, clicks and positions; actual pages, product/service eligibility, available budget and valid paid outcomes

## Method steps

1. Retain query, page and dimension values and verify demand the business can serve in this market. Separate branded/navigation/information/transaction tasks; position and CTR describe only visible data in scope
2. Check impressions/click denominators, periods and page. Position above ten with high CTR can be a course candidate, but sparse impressions or priority changing with one click supports observation. A percentage does not replace demand/cost checks
3. Relevant demand with missing page answers/actions calls for organic content improvement first. A usable page, paid eligibility, cost objective and available input support a bounded paid test. Unserviceable, ineligible or unaffordable cases do not enter paid candidates
4. Prioritize candidates directly matching the current business task and page; specify cap, valid outcome and window. Without paid results, retain test eligibility rather than convert organic CTR into predicted CPA or purchases
5. Deliver query, evidence and organic improvement/paid test/observation/exclusion with revision conditions. Rechoose after mature paid outcomes, page changes, more evidence or eligibility corrections. Paid purchase terms can inform content while source distinctions remain

## Calculation and interpretation

CTR is same-scope clicks/impressions and is undefined at zero impressions. Organic position/CTR do not replace paid cost/purchase evidence

Hypothetical check: position 12 and high CTR, but no matching service page, support fixing the destination rather than buying traffic. An actual serviceable page, eligibility and approved test spend later support a bounded test. Mature acquisition costs above the task target withdraw paid eligibility while preserving the content demand
