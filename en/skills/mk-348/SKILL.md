# MK-348 | Brand Directory Fit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-348.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Platforms worth adding listings to and platforms that do not fit**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Using normal industry practice, inventory public verification locations where operating brands should appear, such as Google Business Profile, company registration, 104 / 1111, associations / trade groups, platforms / channels, and physical stores
2. Do not require the same platforms for every brand; choose places where credible brands in that industry normally appear
3. The purpose is to publicly verify the brand's existence and operations, rather than maximize the number of platforms

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
