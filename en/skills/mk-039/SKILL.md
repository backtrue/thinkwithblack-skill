# MK-039 | Search-need mapping

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-039.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A verifiable path table from event to action**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: The product or service, customers' own words, existing pages, and actions the company can support

## Method

1. Build the need-to-conversion path as "event → search → intent → ad answer → page → action"
2. Each cell must answer the question left by the preceding cell; mark it usable, needs revision, insufficient evidence, or currently unsupported by the company
3. Use the funnel to check filtering and continuity, not to predict that everyone follows a fixed sequence

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need

## Verification cases

**MK-039-C05 | An ad promotes a service that the page does not explain**

Expected: Record a verifiable promise gap; do not immediately conclude it caused all abandonment

Expected: Produce only a need map awaiting validation, not a claimed finding from actual research
