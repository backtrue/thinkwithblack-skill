# MK-155｜Unboxing Collaboration Brief

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-155.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Situations, questions, and evidence to demonstrate**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Provide: actual reviews, customer service records, collaboration content, permissions, and performance data

## Method Steps

1. Clearly specify the target search topic, Persona, product situation, required content format, and requirements for long-term commercial/ad usage permission
2. If the SERP contains many blogger/video/media results, prioritize creators in those results or the same content formats
3. Do not ask partners to invent experiences; retain authentic experiences and the scope of usage permission in third-party content

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement
