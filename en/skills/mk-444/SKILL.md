# MK-444 | Brand Background Brief

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-444.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A fact sheet covering the brand, products, audiences, and limitations**

Maintain, observe, and gather evidence first are valid decisions; do not conceal a lack of evidence behind a well-organized report

## Required inputs

Provide: Brand name, official URL, products/services, audience, approved facts and sources, and names that could be confused

## Method steps

1. Organize existing information into the brand identity section of the brand AI search kit: name, website, one-sentence and three-sentence descriptions, main products / services, main audiences, and incorrect descriptions to avoid
2. Organize only the supplied content; do not conduct a new analysis
3. Mark missing items as “Insufficient data” and list the information needed

## Calculation and interpretation

This task only inventories work; old specifications are not new authorization, and tools must actually be available before claiming execution
