# MK-421 | Monthly Member Newsletter Organization

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-421.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Topics and order for reusable content**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Reorganize the month's social educational resources, KOL / KOC roundups and comparisons, and marketing-theme content into a member newsletter
2. Select newsletter recipients according to member segmentation and the current task; do not send unchanged social content to all members
3. End the newsletter with a next step members can take now, such as repurchasing, viewing an exclusive campaign, or reading further content

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
