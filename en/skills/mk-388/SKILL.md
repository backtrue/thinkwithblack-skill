# MK-388 | New Member First-Purchase Nurturing

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-388.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A message sequence combining education with reasons for a first purchase**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. Design a first-purchase entry point for registered non-buyers / new customers; the course materials suggest a lower-priced starter bundle exclusive to first purchases
2. Travel kits, small trial sets, free-shipping bundles, or first-purchase credits can lower the barrier to buying for the first time
3. This plan is for people who have interacted with the brand but have not bought; do not mix them with F1 members who have already purchased

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
