# MK-182｜Browsing-Stage Educational Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-182.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Copy that builds understanding and trust**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. Build copy for browsing nonbuyers around the hypotheses that they simply have not bought or do not trust the brand
2. Test genuine scarcity/urgency for those who simply have not bought; test third-party testimonials, reviews, or customer service FAQs for insufficient trust
3. Remarketing creative must differ from the original acquisition creative and provide a new reason to buy

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
