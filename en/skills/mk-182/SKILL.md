# MK-182｜Browsing-Stage Educational Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-182.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Copy that builds understanding and trust**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Product facts to explain, page content, available service/usability evidence, stock and shipping/payment conditions; audience definitions and real offer conditions only when used

## Method Steps

1. Inspect page, service, stock, shipping/payment and usability evidence for views without purchases
2. Separate missing information, poor fit, stockouts, flow defects, price conditions and trust hypotheses; use testimonials or genuine urgency only when corresponding evidence supports them
3. For unknown causes, deliver hypotheses and next evidence; tie each message to a supported obstacle and actual destination

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
