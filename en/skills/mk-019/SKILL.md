# MK-019 | Customer pain-point inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-019.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A list of customer problems, their impacts, and evidence**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Use Problem and Implication in SPIN to organize the problems that block a Persona during comparison/decision-making and the consequences of leaving them unresolved
2. Make pain points specific to a situation and comparison point; avoid generic lines such as "Modern life is stressful"
3. Pain points are only part of a value proposition; connect them back to Need-Payoff and the product's actual Benefit

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior

## Verification cases

**MK-019-C05 | An order contains maternity and baby products**

Expected: Do not immediately identify the buyer as a mother; separate the buying and using roles

**MK-019-C06 | Different clues support two situations**

Expected: Keep both candidates and request the data that would distinguish them
