# MK-211｜Cross-Audience Testing of Winning Creative

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-211.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A trackable cross-audience validation plan**

Past success supports candidates and hypotheses only; shared content elements are not proven causes of success

## Required Inputs

Provide: Post IDs, cross-audience performance, creative, and seed-list quality

## Method Steps

1. Test validated Hero Posts across audiences to see whether CTR, engagement, and conversions hold up
2. Separate audience tests into different ad sets; do not mix all labels into one group
3. Posts that continue winning across audiences have greater scaling value; if they fail, revisit audience/task differences

## Calculation and Interpretation

Aggregate by Post ID and distinguish advertising contexts; high engagement or high ROAS alone does not guarantee scalability
