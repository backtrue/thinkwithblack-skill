# MK-422 | Evergreen and Campaign Post Mix

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-422.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Content roles for different tasks**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. The course materials provide no fixed percentage for evergreen versus campaign posts
2. First preserve four content roles: educational resources, third-party roundups and comparisons, the month's marketing theme, and reuse of previously successful content
3. When increasing the campaign theme's share during a promotion, retain education and third-party content so the whole month does not become promotions alone
4. Set actual proportions from the month's campaigns, resources, and historical results; do not invent fixed ratios

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
