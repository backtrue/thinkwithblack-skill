# MK-126｜Third-Party Testimonial Section

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-126.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Only authentic feedback with permission for use**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. Place third-party testimonials in the seventh cell of the nine-cell framework, and keep that section updated
2. Testimonials can come from ordinary users' experiences, authentic platform reviews, awards/certifications, media interviews, or creator content; a major KOL is not required
3. Do not use incentives to fabricate five-star reviews; third-party testimonials must be traceable to authentic sources

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
