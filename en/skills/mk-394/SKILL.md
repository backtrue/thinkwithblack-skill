# MK-394 | High-Potential Member Upgrade Plan

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-394.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Arrangements for moving to the next purchase stage**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Raw inputs: approved RFM rules/states, valid transactions, product cycles, actual preferences/issues, contact eligibility, candidate products/benefits with full costs, stock and existing promises

## Method steps

1. Verify period, scores and approved rules for the high-potential label while retaining actual purchases/preferences. If the next need is unknown, prepare product/service candidates and questions rather than automatically choose discounts
2. Match observed friction: service/education for use issues, trials for aligned untried needs, related products for observed related demand, and discounts only for demonstrated price friction with affordable terms. Recheck needs after purchases, returns or before the product cycle is due
3. Exclude noncontactable, ineligible, unavailable or promise-breaking options. Count discounts, gifts, shipping, processing and fulfillment costs once. Valid demand plus positive available contribution supports bounded candidates; compare costs/landing for the same need
4. Review the same eligible cohort, valid second orders and full costs. Redemptions are observations. Incremental outcomes beyond normal repeat purchases require a matched comparison; redemption orders alone are not all campaign-generated
5. Deliver group needs, choice evidence, costs, plan and revision conditions. Update for transactions, refunds, preferences, eligibility or costs. Members in a new purchase state receive the current-need plan rather than the old-label offer

## Calculation and interpretation

Candidate available contribution equals specified revenue minus product/fulfillment costs and additional benefit costs, each counted once. This checks affordability; incremental outcomes require their own comparison

Hypothetical check: a member has demand for both a discount and trial. Pre-discount contribution 40 minus discount cost 50 excludes the discount; a fulfillable trial retains contribution 60 after all costs, supporting a bounded trial. A later use issue changes the action to service/education
