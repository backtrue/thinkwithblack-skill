# MK-394 | High-Potential Member Upgrade Plan

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-394.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Arrangements for moving to the next purchase stage**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. For high-potential members, use product or campaign preferences to stimulate purchase interest and move them toward higher-value status
2. Course examples include limited-time offers on related individual items, discount codes, and early trials of classic products
3. Select campaigns using actual preferences and RFM segments first; do not treat every high-potential member as having the same need

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
