# MK-119 | Campaign performance review

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-119.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Revenue, profit, contact-list, and repeat-purchase results**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs


Available raw records: original campaign goals, audiences and UTMs, dated spend, valid orders, refunds, costs, and comparable campaigns

## Method Steps

1. Retain objectives, audiences, UTMs, schedules, spend, valid orders, and refunds by campaign. Compare equal stages, observation lengths, and revenue definitions; separate pending conversions or refunds
2. Sales objectives use valid buyers/orders and actual contribution; list objectives use qualified, serviceable contacts and their cost; awareness objectives use the original reach task. High revenue per session does not substitute for every objective
3. Mature results meeting the objective with affordable costs and fulfillment support reuse. A missed objective with a located destination, eligibility, or cost problem supports changing that part. Mature costs beyond the limit with no supported improvement support stopping that offer
4. Describe concurrent price, campaign, or source-mix changes separately. Without a comparable control, report observed results rather than calling a before/after difference incremental impact
5. Replace the reuse or stop recommendation when recovered orders, refunds, or costs change the result. Without an objective, deliver known costs and outcomes and request only the original target needed for the choice

## Calculation and Interpretation

Session value = net revenue / sessions within the same scope. Campaign contribution = valid-order net revenue − product/variable fulfillment costs − campaign and acquisition costs, without double-counting discounts. Deduplicate orders across channels

Check example (hypothetical): an objective of at least 5,000 contribution and a mature estimate of 8,000 support reuse. Refund adjustments reduce contribution to 2,000; withdraw the target-met recommendation and examine the returned items and repairable causes
