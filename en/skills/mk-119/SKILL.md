# MK-119 | Campaign performance review

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-119.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Revenue, profit, contact-list, and repeat-purchase results**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Preserve UTM/campaign classifications for every campaign and maintain long-term records of new users, total revenue, sessions, and value per session
2. Compare traffic value across campaign types to identify which type is more likely to generate revenue from a session
3. Campaigns become comparable history only after they finish. Strong performance once does not guarantee the same future result; keep recording results over time

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
