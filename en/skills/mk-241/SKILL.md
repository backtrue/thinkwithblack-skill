# MK-241｜Improve Product Pages Using Search Terms

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-241.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Needs unanswered by existing pages**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Transactional/action queries and sources, product/service page text/URL, product facts, pricing/eligibility and supported action; existing performance may inform priority

## Method Steps

1. For transaction/action terms, first check whether product, service, or booking pages directly answer price, specifications, eligibility, offers, and the CTA
2. If high-intent terms already bring traffic but the page does not answer them, improve the destination page first rather than only raising bids
3. The course's 7-day action list recommends selecting 3 high-intent terms and rewriting landing pages or FAQs as a minimal complete cycle

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
