# MK-430 | Organic and Paid Traffic Definition Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-430.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Classification evidence and items that cannot be determined directly**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Provide: GA4, GSC, advertising, and sales exports, metric definitions, and costs

## Method steps

1. In the GA4 new-user report, first use source / medium to distinguish direct, referral, cpc, organic, and manual UTM
2. Split both new-customer and returning-customer traffic into organic and paid; the course materials give common examples that brands may adapt to actual needs
3. Direct does not necessarily mean a typed URL; traffic from apps, email software, SMS, or other sources that cannot be tracked correctly may also fall into direct, so retain this limitation in interpretation

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify data sources, dates, currencies, denominators, and attribution; sums across platforms do not equal unique reach or incremental impact
