# MK-285｜In-stream Version Restructuring

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-285.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Storyboard plans for skippable viewing contexts**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Without evidence of reading the video or audio, provide recommendations only for the available text; do not invent video content

## Required Inputs

Media task, audience, product facts/evidence, main message, assets and destination; new storyboards do not require prior performance

## Method Steps

1. With the current skip option after five seconds, establish an identifiable problem/benefit before skipping and develop proof/action afterward
2. Write a shot table with timing, visuals, narration/captions, proof and CTA; use available assets and brief missing ones
3. Check duration, readability, conditions and destination, then confirm fit with current official specifications and preview

## Calculation and Interpretation

Timing is a storyboard estimate to check by read-through/preview; use current official format specifications

Source: [Google: Video ad formats](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464?hl=en)
