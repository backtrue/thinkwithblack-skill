# MK-402 | LINE Interest Tag Design

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-402.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Interest tags linked to content and products**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Interest tags record products / content friends have viewed to make the next message more relevant
2. Create only interest tags that affect subsequent messages or business actions; do not accumulate tags merely for attractive categories
3. Tags must trace back to actual behavior or user-provided information; do not infer sensitive or nonexistent interests

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
