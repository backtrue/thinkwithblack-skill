# MK-233｜Search Term Intent Labeling

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-233.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **The problem each search is trying to solve now**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. First reconstruct what the person is asking now, then label the intent stage
2. The course uses four levels: problem, solution, comparison/selection, and transaction/action
3. At each level, ask what they know, what they fear most, and what you want them to do next; do not crudely rank only by distance from a sale

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
