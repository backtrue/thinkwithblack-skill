# MK-130｜Product Page and Campaign Page Roles

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-130.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **The boundary between ongoing and campaign-specific information**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Product/campaign page content and URLs, traffic sources/jobs, actual campaign conditions and deadline

## Method Steps

1. Product pages describe products and brand qualities for ongoing traffic; campaign pages accelerate orders from engaged nonbuyers and existing customers during limited-time campaigns
2. Product pages usually focus on individual products and value propositions; campaign pages commonly emphasize bundles, multiple products, and price/offer propositions
3. Do not send all annual campaign traffic to ordinary product pages; use the relevant campaign page as the destination

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
