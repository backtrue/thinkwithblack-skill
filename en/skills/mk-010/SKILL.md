# MK-010 | Emotional value proposition

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-010.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Source-supported emotional benefits and communication boundaries**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. Use personal/emotional jobs in JTBD to answer "Can this make me feel better or help me avoid something unpleasant?"
2. Ground emotional value in specific everyday situations, such as peace of mind, comfort, or avoiding unpleasant cleaning, rather than a vague sense of happiness
3. Pair emotional benefits with functional jobs so the product's capabilities support them

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-010-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-010-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
