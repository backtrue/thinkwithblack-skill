# MK-250｜Product Data Field Inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-250.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Gaps in required and commercially necessary fields**

When IDs cannot be matched one-to-one, leave them unmatched rather than guess; do not invent a match rate without complete event samples

## Required Inputs

Provide: product Feed, actual website information, SKU performance, and disapproval messages

## Method Steps

1. Inventory at least the core product catalog fields id, title, description, availability, condition, price, sale_price, link, image_link, brand, and category
2. When using a Feed, retain available fields such as product categories, additional images, GTIN, and mobile links
3. Field values must come from actual product data; do not invent them just to pass catalog formatting

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use product data for specifications and prices; verify catalog fields and disapproval rules during production
