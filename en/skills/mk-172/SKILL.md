# MK-172｜Remarketing Funnel Inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-172.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Segments for browsing, adding to cart, and purchasing**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. Inventory the remarketing funnel using ViewContent → AddToCart → Purchase
2. Calculate the actual VC→ATC and ATC→Purchase ratios to identify whether the problem is closer to browsing without adding to cart or adding to cart without completing purchase
3. Funnel differences identify a break, not the psychological cause; combine customer service, page information, and creative hypotheses to investigate causes

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
