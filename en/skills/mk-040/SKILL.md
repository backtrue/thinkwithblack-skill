# MK-040 | Matching needs to ad formats

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-040.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Task allocation across search, video, and other formats**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: The product or service, customers' own words, existing pages, and actions the company can support

## Method

1. First ask where the customer is and what needs answering, then choose the ad format. Ad formats are not fixed funnel stages
2. Search ads address questions already expressed; video suits demonstrations/explanations; Demand Gen can raise/address needs during content browsing; PMax requires a definition of valuable conversions and a supporting process first
3. Mark the choice undetermined when information is insufficient; do not select PMax merely because the company says it wants more orders

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need
