# MK-022 | Expanding scenarios with 5W1H

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-022.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A table of people, tasks, times, places, and reasons**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Use 5W1H to place the Persona in a short online-purchase story describing Who, When, Where, What, Why, and How
2. Base the story on known product information, USPs, values, motivations, and willingness-to-pay clues; do not invent a life history
3. Use the scenario story to support audience, creative, and page communication; do not treat it as real personal data

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior

## Verification cases

**MK-022-C05 | An order contains maternity and baby products**

Expected: Do not immediately identify the buyer as a mother; separate the buying and using roles

**MK-022-C06 | Different clues support two situations**

Expected: Keep both candidates and request the data that would distinguish them
