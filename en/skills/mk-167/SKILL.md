# MK-167｜Meta Single-Variable Test Check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-167.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Risks of changing the audience and creative at the same time**

Use actual tools and current official specifications to establish platform options and placement availability; old course screenshots do not establish current capabilities

## Required Inputs

Provide: account configuration snapshots, business goals, creative, events, and authorization information

## Method Steps

1. Do not change audience, copy, creative, and placements all at once, or the source of CTR differences cannot be identified
2. First fix the Persona/ad set and compare copy/creative directions; when testing audiences, use separate ad sets
3. The course provides no statistically significant sample size or automatic stopping rules; do not invent them as course methods

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify current platform settings and available audiences; this catalog does not establish an existing account read/write integration
