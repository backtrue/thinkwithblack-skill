# MK-137｜Three-Line Ad Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-137.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A draft covering the situation, connection to the solution, and expected feeling**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: product, audience hypothesis, evidence, landing page, and placement requirements

## Method Steps

1. Use the confirmed three USPs and Persona to produce three distinct directions of three-line copy, and test all three
2. Follow the course's three-line structure: first condense the Persona's situation through Who / When / Where / What, then answer Why, then move to How / the feeling after resolution or the desirable future
3. If fuller persuasion is needed, append long-form FABE copy after the three lines; three lines do not replace all long-form copy

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
