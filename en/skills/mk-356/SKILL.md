# MK-356 | External-to-Website Referral Path Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-356.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **The path from off-site content to node pages and transaction pages**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. External content should ultimately link back to a node page that supports its topic, keeping attention from dispersing
2. First check for an appropriate node page on the site; improve and link to an existing one, or create one first if absent; do not direct traffic to a homepage unable to meet the need
3. Check consistency among the role of external content, anchor / link, and node-page answer to form a Barnacle SEO path of “position→asset→referral”

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
