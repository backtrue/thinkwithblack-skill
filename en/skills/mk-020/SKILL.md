# MK-020 | Distinguishing needs from wants

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-020.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A comparison of necessary solutions and desired feelings**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Distinguish "the job that needs to be done" from "the desired experience/identity"; JTBD separates functional, social, and emotional jobs
2. High/low need describes need intensity; do not equate low need directly with having no motivation to buy
3. Assess both needs and wants within specific situations and purchase frequencies so they remain grounded in context

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
