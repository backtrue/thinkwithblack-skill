# MK-273｜Match Demand Gen Creative to Channels

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-273.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Creative requirements for different viewing contexts**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Views or clicks do not automatically stand in for sales; do not directly rank channels measured on different bases

## Required Inputs

Planned channels/viewing contexts, readable assets or dimensions, main message, CTA and destination

## Method Steps

1. Describe orientation, sound, first-screen and readability needs per context and inspect available assets
2. Map assets and locate cropping, caption, overlay, pacing or message gaps
3. Deliver a specific production brief and preview checks for each gap, verifying current official format specifications during production

## Calculation and Interpretation

With only a transcript, inspect wording/order and leave image, cropping and sound unobserved
