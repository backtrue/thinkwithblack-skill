# MK-234｜Search Term Mining

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-234.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Demand candidates worth serving independently**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. Mining identifies high-intent, high-converting search terms worth serving independently
2. Actions extend beyond adding keywords to creating new campaigns, ad groups, or landing pages
3. Before deciding, inspect the question behind the term, its distance from purchase, and whether the current page answers it; without conversion tracking, high clicks do not establish high value

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
