## Required Inputs

Video×placement/format detail, audiences, periods, delivery opportunity, viewing/actions, video content and goal

## Method Steps

1. Split video×placement data, labeling audience/window differences and cells without delivery
2. Compare videos within audience/format and audiences within video/format; identify missing cross-cells that prevent isolation
3. List retention, context-fit and audience-fit hypotheses, each with evidence and a discriminating next test

## Calculation and Interpretation

Aggregate results locate questions, not proven causes
