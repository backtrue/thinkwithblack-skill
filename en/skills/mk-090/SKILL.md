# MK-090 | Product-profitability prioritization

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-090.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A comparison of revenue, gross margin, and conversion performance**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: SKUs, product costs, customer groups, purchase records, and product lines

## Method

1. Obtain product revenue, items purchased, and items viewed from GA's e-commerce purchase report, then add product gross margin ratios
2. Product conversion rate = items purchased ÷ items viewed
3. Earnings index = product revenue × gross margin ratio × conversion rate. Rank by relative values to identify products better suited to direct advertising, SEO, or blogger content
4. This index is a relative decision metric used in the course; it is not complete accounting profit

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
