# MK-332 | Firsthand Experience Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-332.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Evidence of experience and gaps that cannot be filled by invention**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Check whether the article contains any firsthand material the author / brand has seen, done, tested, served, or observed
2. Firsthand experience is more than a claim of expertise; it may come from customer service, sales, customer reviews, cases, or KOL experiences
3. If the entire article consists of definitions, compilations, and paraphrases, mark firsthand experience as insufficient

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
