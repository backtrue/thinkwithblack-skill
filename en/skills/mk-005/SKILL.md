# MK-005 | Selling-point evidence matching

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-005.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A claim, evidence, and gap comparison table**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. For Evidence in FABE, use only genuine proof, cases, or data that can build trust
2. If a USP is strengthened with numbers, verify their sources first; retain a nonnumeric version if no source is available
3. Evidence should substantiate the Feature/Advantage/Benefit. Do not fill the space with irrelevant awards or vague appeals to authority

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-005-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-005-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
