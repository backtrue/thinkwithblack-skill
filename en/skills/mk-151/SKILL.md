# MK-151｜Review Creative Permission Check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-151.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A list of sources, usage permissions, and sensitive information**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Provide: actual reviews, customer service records, collaboration content, permissions, and performance data

## Method Steps

1. Before using blogger/KOL/KOC Page content, confirm that the brand has usage rights or has obtained the relevant permission
2. When using reviews or comments on the brand's own sales page, Page, or business profile, also retain the original source and follow existing usage permissions
3. Do not turn third-party content directly into ad creative without permission

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement

## Verification Cases

**MK-151-C05｜Data meets existing conditions**

Expected: report usable or keep as is; do not invent a need for changes

Expected: report data not obtained; do not classify the tool error as a business anomaly
