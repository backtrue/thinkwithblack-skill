# MK-268｜Demand Generation Audience Hypotheses

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-268.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Audience candidates built from existing evidence**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Views or clicks do not automatically stand in for sales; do not directly rank channels measured on different bases

## Required Inputs

Customer wording, queries or purchase signals, product limits and need triggers

## Method Steps

1. Extract needs, contexts and behavior signals with sources
2. Group signals with the same job into candidate audiences, separating facts, inference and counterexamples
3. Specify fit, creative angle and validation question; age or an interest label alone does not establish motivation

## Calculation and Interpretation

Prioritize by evidence and fit, retaining hypotheses until tested
