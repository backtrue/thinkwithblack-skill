# MK-083 | Product progression from first to repeat purchase

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-083.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **The product path after the first purchase**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs


Available raw records: first-purchase SKU, quantity, purpose and use, available products and costs, later orders, and product cycles

## Method Steps

1. Use first SKU, quantity, purpose, usage conditions, and later orders to find the next unmet need. For directional advice, propose conditional paths from existing products without requiring complete second/third-purchase metrics
2. Choose replenishment for a consumable approaching an evidenced usage cycle. Choose cross-product continuation when a compatible accessory or next-stage need exists while the main product remains usable. Do not force an unrelated, already-owned, or incompatible margin product
3. For both second and third purchases, ask what the previous order resolved and what remains needed. Exclude already purchased or unsuitable products, then inspect stock, contribution, and contact eligibility
4. Time replenishment from actual quantity/use or product-specific intervals; time complementary products from the usage stage. Without cycle data, give a trigger and request product-use evidence rather than imposing 10–14 days
5. Replace the next product or date after a new order, remaining stock, changed use, or return. If the planned second product has been bought, move only to the next evidenced need

## Calculation and Interpretation

When measurement is requested, next-product purchase rate = people in the same first-purchase cohort buying that product within the same horizon / the complete original first-buyer group, including nonrepeat buyers. Report product mix among repeat buyers separately. Deliver product, fit evidence, trigger, exclusions, and revision record for each step

Check example (hypothetical): a printer buyer explicitly needs duplex binding and a compatible binding accessory exists, supporting a next-step candidate. A new order shows that accessory was bought; remove the promotion and schedule consumable replenishment only when actual usage supports it

## Activation conditions and full question scope

Use this skill directly when the user wants follow-on purchase directions for existing customers or a progression from the first purchase to other brand products, including second and third purchases. The original method's ongoing purchases are not limited to the second order. Propose paths using the store's products, needs, and purchase data; a separate method named "third purchase" is not required

Real question Q005: "Sales have stalled, few customers buy a second or third time, and I don't want to keep increasing advertising investment. What direction could I take?" [Original question](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-i-increase-repeat-customer-sales/271013/1). Preserve the constraint against continually increasing ads. A product progression is one applicable operating direction; the author's belief that ads are already optimized remains a self-report

Check SKUs, product costs, customer groups, purchase records, and product lines first. If unavailable, use the seven required fields to deliver conditional candidates, evidence of fit, and missing inputs; do not invent products, gross margins, cycles, or sales outcomes. Unless precise measurement is requested, do not make full-history segmentation or second-/third-purchase-rate calculations mandatory prerequisites

If the task is only to arrange a second product and reminder timing for someone who has bought once, use the first-buyer second-purchase planning method. If the request also includes cause diagnosis, actual repeat-purchase metrics, causal effects, loyalty programs, or platform configuration, retain every task and find the applicable methods; product progression cannot substitute for them. General operating advice is not a request to prove revenue has already increased; this skill does not promise an outcome
