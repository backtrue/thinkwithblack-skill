# MK-006 | Prioritizing three lead selling points

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-006.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Lead selling-point priorities and reasons for trade-offs**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. Narrow the lead selling points to three. The course material's rationale is that more selling points mean lower profit and less focused communication
2. Let the AI identify candidates first, then discuss/vote using exclusivity, personalization, quantification, and relevance to the Persona
3. Feed the ranking back into the Persona/copy workflow; prioritize these three points on the product page and in FABE

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-006-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-006-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
