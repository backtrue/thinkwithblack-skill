# MK-056 | Supplementary questions for scenarios and roles

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-056.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Questions that resolve uncertainty in role markers**

Without raw survey responses, produce only a questionnaire or analysis plan; do not fabricate answers

## Required inputs

Provide the purchase/usage context to clarify, known buyer/user clues, the subsequent recommendation decision, and available questioning channel. Include original responses when they already exist

## Method

1. Because contextual role-marker products identify roles less strongly, design follow-up questions to establish the life situation and next need
2. Place the questions in surveys, email-marketing tags, or remarketing interactions to establish the actual role behind the purchase
3. Ask only for information that affects subsequent product recommendations; do not turn the questionnaire into unrelated demographic-data collection

## Calculations and interpretation

Separate purchaser, user, and gift recipient. Tie each follow-up to an unresolved fact that changes the recommendation. Retain alternatives when an answer has multiple interpretations; do not infer identity from nonresponse or collect unrelated personal details

## Verification cases

**MK-056-A1 | Baby products were purchased but intended use is unknown**

Expected: Ask about self-use, gifts, or proxy purchase when relevant to the recommendation; do not label the buyer a parent

**MK-056-A2 | The respondent declines to answer**

Expected: Keep the role unknown and use known product needs
