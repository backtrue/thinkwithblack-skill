# MK-338 | Article Next-Step Entry Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-338.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Reasonable actions and page links**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. End the article with a next step consistent with the question addressed, such as a checklist, further reading, tool, or case page
2. The next step should meet the reader's current need, rather than force in a generic sales CTA
3. If the article helps readers understand a problem, the next step should follow logically instead of jumping to an unrelated product

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
