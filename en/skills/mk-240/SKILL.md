# MK-240｜Turn Search Terms into Comparison Pages

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-240.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Comparison needs and what should be compared**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. Comparison/selection terms suit comparison pages or content, such as “recommendations,” “which type fits,” or “A vs B”
2. Explain selection criteria, differences, and applicable situations rather than presenting your brand as the only answer
3. If high-intent comparison terms convert well, consider a dedicated ad group and destination page

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone

## Verification Cases

**MK-240-C05｜Comparison periods have different lengths or definitions**

Expected: do not directly compare totals; first list items that are not comparable and those that can safely be compared
