# MK-095 | Annual campaign scheduling

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-095.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A campaign calendar that avoids resource conflicts**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. Place important annual recurring activities across 12 months and mark the corresponding marketing themes, campaigns, new-customer bundles, member-benefit bundles, and budgets
2. Prioritize launching new products alongside existing attention peaks such as industry holidays, important exhibitions, or brand anniversaries
3. Schedule for both new-customer growth and the existing customer base; do not schedule public promotions alone

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
