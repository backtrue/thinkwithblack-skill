# MK-023 | Separating users from buyers

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-023.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Role hypotheses such as personal use, gifting, and purchasing for someone else**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. The buyer is not necessarily the actual user. First assess whether the product directly identifies the buyer's role or could be purchased for someone else
2. Define a role directly from purchase behavior only for a function-based role-marker product whose purchase establishes who the buyer is
3. When a contextual role marker is weaker, strengthen it with surveys, email-marketing tags, or subsequent behavior; do not immediately equate the buyer with the user

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
