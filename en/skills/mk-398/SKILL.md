# MK-398 | Member Campaign Repeat Purchase Review

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-398.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Repeat purchase results and confounding factors for the same group**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. Set independent KPIs for every CRM campaign; do not combine different member groups or campaigns in one calculation
2. After the campaign, observe goal attainment and member movement for that list
3. CRM is dynamic; update lists monthly before determining whether members moved to other tiers
4. One campaign result does not guarantee future effectiveness; the course materials require ongoing testing and optimization

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
