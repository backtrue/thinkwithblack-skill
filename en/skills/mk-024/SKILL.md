# MK-024 | Time-based scenario angles

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-024.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A scenario question bank for usage times or seasons**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Use Time in T.P.O. to consider how willingness to pay changes with timing, seasons, high/low months, and seasonal exclusivity
2. First identify when demand/willingness to pay is higher, then design corresponding bundles or messages; do not use holidays solely as reasons for promotions
3. Support time-based angles with brand or market data. Course examples are illustrative and should not be applied directly to other industries

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
