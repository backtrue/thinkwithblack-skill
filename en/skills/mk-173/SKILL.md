# MK-173｜Diagnose Browsing Without Adding to Cart

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-173.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Possible causes to verify, including insufficient information and demand mismatch**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. For people with ViewContent but no AddToCart/Purchase, first test two hypotheses: they simply have not ordered/need an urgency reminder, or they lack brand trust/need third-party testimonials
2. Do not directly interpret browsing without adding to cart as a price problem; the course identifies trust and simply not having bought as the primary copy directions
3. Test limited-quantity/urgency creative separately from review-based creative to see which better advances the next event

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
