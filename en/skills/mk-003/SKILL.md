# MK-003 | Selling-point distinctiveness check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-003.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A comparison of generic slogans and genuine differences**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. Check the USP from the course material's three angles: exclusivity, personalization, and quantification
2. If the same USP also fits other products, judge it insufficiently distinctive
3. Quantification may use only genuine, provable numbers; do not invent data to make the claim more persuasive

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Activation conditions and full question scope

Use this skill directly when the user wants to assess an existing selling point, USP, or differentiation claim and receive a comparison of generic slogans with verifiable differences. First obtain the claim, product facts, intended customers, and readable competitor information. Even with insufficient competitor data, identify generic wording; leave exclusivity pending verification

If "How do we beat competitors?" or "Find our market positioning from scratch" also involves market research, new-product opportunities, customer-review collection, or competitive strategy, check the capability needed for each part. Checking a single claim does not complete the whole question. If the user merely mentions a competitor's name, first establish whether they actually want an existing claim checked

## Verification cases

**MK-003-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-003-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
