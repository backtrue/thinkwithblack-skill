# MK-028 | Persona evidence review

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-028.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A checklist distinguishing supported items from invented assumptions**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Persona or need hypotheses to check, product facts, customer wording, survey or service records and sources

## Method

1. Treat Persona/need hypotheses as candidate answers initially; AI generation does not make them market facts
2. Use actual evidence such as interviews, search terms, customer-service records, and existing pages to check customer problems, searches, and next steps
3. If the AI invents an unheard-of problem, a page the company does not have, or an action customers may not want to take, mark insufficient evidence or the company's current inability to support it

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior

## Verification cases

**MK-028-C05 | An order contains maternity and baby products**

Expected: Do not immediately identify the buyer as a mother; separate the buying and using roles

**MK-028-C06 | Different clues support two situations**

Expected: Keep both candidates and request the data that would distinguish them
