For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-322.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Items to check, including language, canonical, and robots**

Permission in robots or the presence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine technical website status and content quality into one conclusion

## Required inputs

Provide: website pages, HTML, existing content, audience, and search needs

## Method steps

1. Basic head checks: title, lang, Meta Description, Open Graph, Canonical, Meta Robots
2. Basic body checks: H1–H3, strong, ul / ol, table, img alt
3. This is a core structure checklist for content creators; actual indexing / crawling requires separate checks of website and search engine status

## Calculation and interpretation

Actually crawl and read the content and website structure; check current rules and markup, without guaranteeing rankings or citations
