# MK-018 | Grouping purchase motivations

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-018.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Groups with different motivations for buying the same product**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. A purchase motivation is a specific trigger; separate it from underlying values
2. For each Persona, first list specific situations explaining "Why buy now?", then classify them as functional, social, or emotional jobs using JTBD
3. Translate values × purchase motivations into creative language; do not merge every motivation into the same message

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
