# MK-308｜Sitewide Page Type Inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-308.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **An asset table of home, category, product, article, and other pages**

Asset group signals are not equivalent to precise audience targeting; separately verify actual platform capabilities

## Required Inputs

Site URL inventory/readable content, existing page jobs and available audience/search-need evidence

## Method Steps

1. Inventory existing page types, including home, category, article, product/service, brand, FAQ, case study, and booking pages
2. First describe the keywords/search demand each page type served in the SEO era and the current division of roles
3. Then identify pages with an existing SEO foundation and pages needing an upgrade to an AEO arrangement of questions, answers, and hubs

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
