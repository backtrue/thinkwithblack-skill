# MK-002 | Unique selling proposition extraction

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-002.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Evidence-based candidates for differentiated selling points**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. First identify three important USPs from the product information to form the core of the product page, FABE, and subsequent creative materials
2. A USP should explain why the product deserves to exist relative to the current situation or alternatives, rather than using generic adjectives that could apply to any product
3. When the AI identifies the wrong points, correct them through brand feedback, then use discussion/voting to converge on three revised USPs

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-002-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-002-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
