# MK-350 | Brand Media Kit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-350.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Names, descriptions, services, differentiators, and links**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Build a source-material kit for external descriptions: correct brand name, one-sentence / three-sentence introductions, audience, core products / services, suitable contexts, differentiation, official website links, and incorrect descriptions to avoid
2. The kit helps media, KOLs, bloggers, and partners describe the brand accurately; it does not control their writing
3. All content must come from actual brand information; do not add nonexistent positioning

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
