# MK-008 | Turning selling points into usage scenarios

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-008.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Creative angles that place specifications in everyday situations**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Product specifications, competitor differences, verifiable evidence, and intended customers

## Method

1. Use the Persona's Who, When, Where, and What to identify situations in which the product would be used or needed
2. Place the USP in that situation and confirm it addresses the role's immediate problem or desired change
3. The same product may fit different scenarios for different Personas; do not treat one scenario as the only use across the entire market

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-008-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-008-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
