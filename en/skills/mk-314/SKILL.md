# MK-314｜Article-to-Hub Internal Link Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-314.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Paths from content back to commercial hubs**

Permission in robots or the existence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine site technical status and content quality into a single conclusion

## Required Inputs

Actual source-article and target-node content/URLs, reader job and candidate insertion point

## Method Steps

1. Link informational articles back to the relevant topic hub at suitable locations so people still understanding the problem can see the next layer of options
2. Anchor text should describe the next page's task rather than simply say “Learn more”
3. Do not add internal links for their own sake; link only when the hub genuinely addresses the question

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
