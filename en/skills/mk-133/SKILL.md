# MK-133｜Sales Page Creative Breakdown

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-133.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A list of module copy ready for design handoff**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Source page/copy, original section jobs, available images/assets, target channels and verified production specifications

## Method Steps

1. Design the nine-cell framework as individual cells, with a recommended size of 1080×1080 or 2160×2160 per cell, making them reusable as ecommerce images and ad creative
2. Individual cells support reuse across Shopee / Amazon / Facebook / LINE / Google and allow an underperforming cell to be replaced on its own later
3. Preserve each cell's original task during breakdown; do not turn all nine into identical product introduction images

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
