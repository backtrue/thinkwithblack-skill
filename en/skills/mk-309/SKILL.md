# MK-309｜Website Audience Inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-309.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **The audiences and tasks served by each page type**

Permission in robots or the existence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine site technical status and content quality into a single conclusion

## Required Inputs

Provide: website pages, HTML, existing content, audiences, and search demand

## Method Steps

1. Define the website's 3 most likely target audiences, describing identity characteristics, current situation, real problems, reasons for starting a search, and distance from action
2. Audiences are working hypotheses for predicting questions and must later be validated with actual search/customer data
3. Do not treat these three audiences as the website's only customer groups

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
