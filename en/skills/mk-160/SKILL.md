# MK-160｜Translate Business Goals into Meta Ad Tasks

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-160.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Advertising tasks and performance criteria**

Use actual tools and current official specifications to establish platform options and placement availability; old course screenshots do not establish current capabilities

## Required Inputs

Business purpose/intended outcome, destination and available measurement; explain general conditions directly, requesting account snapshots/relevant event data only for specific settings

## Method Steps

1. First translate the business purpose into a Meta campaign objective: Traffic may suit bringing people to content; Sales or another corresponding objective suits generating orders. Keep an existing purpose; if it is unknown, provide conditional candidates first and then obtain that missing item. Do not choose the purpose just because advertising has just begun, clicks are cheap, or LPV is hard to measure. General method questions do not require a real account first
2. Then choose a conversion location such as a website, messaging destination, or Instagram/Facebook according to the actual purpose. List campaign objective, conversion location, and performance goal separately, and check the settings currently available for that combination. For website arrivals, assess the LPV performance goal; clicking a specified link and successfully loading it are different outcomes, so do not treat link clicks as an equivalent substitute
3. The objective affects optimization and reported results/cost per result, so define the purpose before setting up ads. Attach the intended outcome, corresponding measurement, and missing items to each candidate configuration. When investigating differences between clicks and LPV, retain the existing destination and measurement definition; an unexplained gap is not evidence for changing the objective. If a final case-specific choice is needed but the business purpose is missing, leave it pending data; narrow to one recommendation only when information is sufficient

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify current platform settings and available audiences; this catalog does not establish an existing account read/write integration
