# MK-060 | Contribution margin ratio calculation

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-060.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Contribution amounts and revenue ratios with explicit definitions**

Recalculability does not mean the data is complete; break-even points and cost ceilings apply only under the stated assumptions

## Required inputs

Provide: Net revenue and variable costs on the same scope, or verified contribution

For method questions, provide the formula, variables and an example directly; for a calculation, ask only for missing operands

## Method

1. Contribution margin ratio = (revenue − variable costs) ÷ revenue × 100%
2. The course material uses 30–40% as a healthy range and 25% as a floor to try not to fall below. Above 40%, also check whether variable costs have been omitted
3. These are course operating benchmarks; calculations still require the brand's actual costs

## Calculations and interpretation

Contribution margin ratio = contribution margin ÷ net revenue on the same basis

Retain original values, units, periods, and substituted formulas. The formula is not an unconditional guarantee of results

Define gross profit, contribution margin, cost denominators, and periods explicitly; do not copy classroom example numbers as universal thresholds
