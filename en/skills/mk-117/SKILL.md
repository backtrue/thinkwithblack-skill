# MK-117 | Assessing weekend or time-slot campaigns

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-117.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Deciding whether a campaign is needed from time-slot data first**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Check actual sales/conversion data by time slot to establish whether weekends or evenings are genuinely weak
2. If a weak time slot is evident, design a weekend/evening flash campaign to support traffic and conversions; do not schedule promotions based on general impressions alone
3. Still check the contribution margin ratio and average order value; do not use promotions merely to pull demand forward from otherwise normal time slots

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
