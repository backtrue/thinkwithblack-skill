# MK-335 | Expert Quotation Authenticity Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-335.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Checks of quotations, identities, and original sources**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. For expert quotations, first verify that the person / organization, original source, and intended meaning are traceable
2. Do not package a brand's own statements as third-party expert evidence
3. Use a quotation only to support claims it actually covers; do not extend it into conclusions absent from the source

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
