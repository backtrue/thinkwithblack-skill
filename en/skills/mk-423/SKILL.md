# MK-423 | Long-Article Adaptation into Short Social Posts

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-423.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Passage adaptations without new facts**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Original article or specified passages, audience/platform, product facts and conditions to preserve, adaptation purpose and length requirements

## Method steps

1. Select one question the source answers and locate its supporting facts
2. Write a complete standalone short post, retaining conditions/author perspective without turning examples into universal outcomes
3. Check each sentence against the source and remove invented numbers, experiences or causality; deliver the passage mapping and actual post

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
