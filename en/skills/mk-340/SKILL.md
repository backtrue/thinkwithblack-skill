# MK-340 | Four-Sentence Answer Paragraph Draft

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-340.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Answer modules reorganized around the original facts**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Directly answer the question in the first sentence below the H2
2. Explain the reason for that judgment in the second sentence
3. Describe the applicable context in the third sentence
4. Begin examples or elaboration in the fourth sentence; the four-sentence formula is an Answer-First opening, not a four-sentence limit for the whole paragraph

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
