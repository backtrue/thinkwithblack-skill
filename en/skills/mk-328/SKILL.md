# MK-328 | List Module Fit

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-328.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Passages suitable for conversion to lists, with reasons**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Prioritize a list module when answering “What options / items are there?”
2. Each list item should stand on its own; do not bury all the information in long paragraphs
3. Do not force lists into every article; use them when the topic can meaningfully be split into extractable items

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
