# MK-333 | Screenshot, Record, and Data Evidence Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-333.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Required evidence, dates, and missing items**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Experience needs verifiable traces, such as before-and-after cases, actual data, tool screens, process screenshots, on-site photos, timelines, test records, or customer feedback
2. Check whether evidence actually supports the experience described, rather than merely adding decorative images
3. Without evidence, do not present subjective impressions as verified facts

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
