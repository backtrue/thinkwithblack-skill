# MK-185｜New-Customer Offer Reminder Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-185.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Reminders consistent with eligibility and conditions**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. New-customer offers reduce friction before a first order; course examples include new-customer credit, shipping credit, and starter bundles
2. The offer must actually exist and match new-customer eligibility
3. Do not indiscriminately show new-customer offers to previous buyers; apply corresponding remarketing exclusions

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
