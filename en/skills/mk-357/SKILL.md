# MK-357 | Off-Site Correction Outreach Draft

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-357.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Evidence-supported brand correction or supplementary outreach drafts**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Draft outreach only for external pages already inventoried and judged worth contacting
2. Explain the issue in the current description and provide correct brand information / expert views / cases and official links so the recipient can verify the material
3. Do not request paid links, spun content, or forced use of brand talking points; the purpose is to correct misleading context or provide citable information

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
