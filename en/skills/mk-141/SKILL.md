# MK-141｜Feature Demonstration Creative Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-141.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Features and scenes to film or demonstrate**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: product, audience hypothesis, evidence, landing page, and placement requirements

## Method Steps

1. Begin the demonstration with Feature/Advantage so the audience understands how the product works or differs from alternatives
2. Follow the demonstration with the Persona's Benefit; avoid filming specifications alone
3. Plan creative dimensions together with placements: square for feeds, vertical for Stories/Reels; do not force one size into every placement

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
