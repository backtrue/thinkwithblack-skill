# MK-135｜FABE Ad Copy Generation

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-135.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A complete draft covering features, advantages, benefits, and evidence**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: Confirmed Persona and three USPs with evidence; identify missing items and organize what is supplied without inventing replacements

## Method Steps

1. Use the confirmed Persona and three USPs to generate complete FABE copy in the sequence Feature → Advantage → Benefit → Evidence
2. Feature states measurable specifications or unique characteristics; Advantage states a relative advantage; Benefit states a concrete change for the Persona; Evidence provides real proof
3. Use the Persona's language to turn the product into value they care about, rather than simply listing specifications

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
