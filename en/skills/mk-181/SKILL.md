# MK-181｜Remarketing Audience Naming

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-181.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Names identifying events, windows, and exclusions**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Provide: the event funnel, pages, existing audiences, and actual offer terms

## Method Steps

1. Custom audience names should let you and the team quickly understand the source, event, product, and period later
2. Names must distinguish at least VC, ATC, Purchase, website/Page/IG, and the associated product
3. The original course prescribes no unique string format; prioritize readability, maintainability, and clarity

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
