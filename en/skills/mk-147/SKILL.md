# MK-147｜Ad Creative Brief

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-147.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Specifications and evidence requirements for design or filming**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: product, audience hypothesis, evidence, landing page, and placement requirements

## Method Steps

1. Include at least the Persona, three USPs, value proposition, three-line messaging directions to test, and placement dimensions in the brief
2. Prepare dimensions separately for feeds and Reels/Stories, and identify the messaging direction each asset tests
3. Attach FABE when long-form copy is needed; if evidence assets are insufficient, list the missing items explicitly rather than generating fake testimonials

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
