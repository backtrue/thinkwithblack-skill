# MK-092 | Annual marketing-event inventory

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-092.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A table of holidays, brand milestones, and launch events**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. First mark the brand's high/low seasons and important recurring annual activities
2. Recurring activities include anniversary promotions, industry holidays, exhibitions, and events the brand always joins or that regularly occur in the market
3. If the brand lacks experience, the course suggests external industry/e-commerce aggregate data; do not treat external averages as the brand's history

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
