# MK-097 | Educational-traffic budget planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-097.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Resource uses that build understanding before conversion**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. Educational traffic maintains overall brand exposure and builds usable digital assets; it can include KOC, KOL, PR, and related content
2. Plan marketing spend together with maintaining overall exposure, ongoing traffic, and campaign traffic; educational content should not begin only on campaign day
3. The original course material provides no fixed percentage for educational traffic; determine the actual budget from the overall target and contribution margin table

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
