# MK-152｜Review Collage Creative Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-152.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Original excerpts and visual arrangements**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Each original review, source, and date; permission records covering channels, formats, term, and excerpts; permission for names and avatars; intended use, placement, and available assets

## Method Steps

1. Create collage assets from multiple authentic reviewer avatars/experiences to convey social proof and that many people actually use the product
2. Every item must come from an authentic, usable review; do not fill the layout with fake avatars or invented experiences
3. Collages primarily serve trust-focused remarketing; they do not directly replace traffic acquisition creative
4. Map every visual excerpt to the original review and check its channel, term, and excerpt rights; when identity permissions are unknown, mark them for confirmation and use an authorized anonymous version or omit the item
5. Specify hierarchy and positions for the primary review, supporting reviews, and source labels; retain qualifying conditions and do not splice a new meaning; deliver asset and permission status for each item

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement

## Verification Cases

**MK-152-A1 | A review may be quoted but avatar rights are unconfirmed**

Expected: Retain the source and permitted excerpt; mark avatar permission as unknown and do not fill the gap with a fake avatar
