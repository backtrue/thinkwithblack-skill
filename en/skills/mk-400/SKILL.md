# MK-400 | Add-Friend Flow Friction Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-400.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Friction in claiming benefits and taking the next step**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Check whether joining offers immediate access to the incentive with little friction; avoid unnecessary steps that cause abandonment
2. Check whether the incentive relates to the user's current context / pain point and whether the next step is clear after joining
3. Do not treat low CPF as proof of a successful flow; also examine post-join interactions, first purchases, and repeat purchases

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
