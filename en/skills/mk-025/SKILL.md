# MK-025 | Place-based scenario angles

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-025.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Differences in needs across settings**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Use Place in T.P.O. to consider how audiences, selling costs, and willingness to pay differ across channels
2. Design different bundles for the same product across the brand website, e-commerce platforms, auction platforms, and department stores/physical channels to avoid direct price comparison
3. The course material's channel-specific average order values are examples, not fixed benchmarks for every brand

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
