# MK-315｜Hub-to-Product Internal Link Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-315.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Paths from answers to products and services**

Permission in robots or the existence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine site technical status and content quality into a single conclusion

## Required Inputs

Node question/content, intent-maturity evidence, candidate product/service page content/URLs and supported next action

## Method Steps

1. Direct readers from the hub to comparison, case study, offer, product/service, or booking pages according to commercial investigation/transactional intent
2. Match link placement and CTA to readiness; do not push informational users into an action too early
3. Product/service pages must actually answer the question promised by the hub

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
