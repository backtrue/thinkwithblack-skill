# MK-140｜Evidence-Led Creative Planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-140.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A plan for turning data or demonstrations into creative**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: product, audience hypothesis, evidence, landing page, and placement requirements

## Method Steps

1. When Evidence is the main creative angle, use only real cases, data, awards, or other verifiable proof
2. Evidence must support the current USP/Benefit; do not pile up unrelated proof to create an impression of trust
3. If evidence is insufficient, use another creative direction; do not generate fake testimonials or effect sizes

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
