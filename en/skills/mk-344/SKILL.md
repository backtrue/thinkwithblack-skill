# MK-344 | Cross-Platform Brand Introduction Comparison

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-344.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Inconsistencies between the official website and other platforms**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Compare names, descriptions, services, and links across the website's About page, Google Business Profile, FB / IG, LinkedIn, YouTube, and other platforms
2. Find accounts with insufficient descriptions, slogans alone, missing official website links, or unclear services
3. Length may differ by platform, but core positioning and official links should remain consistent

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
