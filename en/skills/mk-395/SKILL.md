# MK-395 | Win-Back Plan for At-Risk Members

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-395.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Recent changes and suitable reminders**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. For loyal members due for reactivation, renew their anticipation of special treatment and exclusivity
2. Course examples include exclusive offers on preferred products, early-bird trials of new products, and member newsletters
3. Use private, targeted marketing channels so member-only offers do not become routine public promotions
4. Update lists monthly; people who have purchased again should leave the original win-back segment

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
