# MK-055 | Product-preference question design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-055.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Questions that distinguish reasons for choosing a product**

Without raw survey responses, produce only a questionnaire or analysis plan; do not fabricate answers

## Required inputs

Provide the decision the research supports, actual product facts, target respondents, and questioning context. Include existing customer wording when available; prior responses are not a prerequisite for designing questions

## Method

1. Break the research decision into purchase reasons, barriers, and usage needs to distinguish; give each question one purpose
2. Write neutral questions from actual product facts and context. Ask about choices or experience before reasons, with one issue per question; do not present brand assumptions as established needs
3. Specify single-choice, multiple-choice, or open responses. Resolve overlapping options, include other/not-applicable when useful, and base skip logic on actual experience
4. Deliver questions with option sources, then check an applicable and a non-applicable scenario for answerability. Use only the user's specified arrangements for lead collection or incentives

## Calculations and interpretation

Questions should distinguish answers that would change the decision. Avoid leading wording, double-barreled questions, and assuming a purchase. Before collection, deliver questions and checks without inventing sample sizes, preference shares, or purchase conclusions

## Verification cases

**MK-055-A1 | Only product specifications are supplied for a post-purchase survey**

Expected: Deliver draft questions, option sources, and response formats without requiring prior responses

**MK-055-A2 | A question asks whether cheap and easy-to-use features caused liking**

Expected: Separate the reasons or use a neutral open question instead of combining two claims
