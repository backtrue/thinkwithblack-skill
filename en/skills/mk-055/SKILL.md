# MK-055 | Product-preference question design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-055.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Questions that distinguish reasons for choosing a product**

Without raw survey responses, produce only a questionnaire or analysis plan; do not fabricate answers

## Required inputs

Provide: Product costs, target audience, raw survey responses, or survey purpose

## Method

1. The questionnaire can contain more questions rather than fewer. First turn the pain points, needs, and product features the brand believes may matter into selectable options
2. Besides research, the survey can serve as a warm-up campaign to collect a first-party contact list; completion may lead to a predefined coupon or follow-up campaign
3. Survey options must come from real products and situations; do not add nonexistent features or benefits

## Calculations and interpretation

Check sampling, valid responses, and the ordering of price questions; willingness to pay is neither actual purchasing nor a profit guarantee
