# MK-153｜Turn Customer Service Q&A into Creative

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-153.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Ad drafts built from real questions and answers**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Actual customer-service questions and answers with sources and dates; verified product or service facts; privacy and quotation permissions; ad objective, intended audience, and destination page

## Method Steps

1. Use the questions most often received by customer service to identify concerns that may block cart adders or browsers from buying
2. Turn genuine Q&A into ad creative that directly answers the reasons consumers hesitate
3. Customer service content provides clues, not proof that every nonbuyer abandoned for the same reason; combine it with events and creative tests
4. Verify that each answer still matches the product, remove identifying information not cleared for use, and deliver usable body copy, headline, CTA, and destination while preserving the original answer’s conditions and limits

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement

## Verification cases

**MK-153-A1 | Customer service confirmed next-day dispatch only for an in-stock size**

Expected: Retain the size, stock, and dispatch conditions; do not promise next-day delivery for every product
