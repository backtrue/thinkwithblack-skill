# MK-014 | Diagnosing unfocused selling points

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-014.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Locations where copy departs from the core value and directions for revision**

Separate features, benefits, and evidence; do not claim exclusivity without competitor data

## Required inputs

Provide: Text or page to review and its location, intended audience and focus, product facts and supporting evidence

## Method

1. If the USP could also apply to other products, identify a lack of focus in exclusivity
2. If the subjects in the copy are only the product and never the user, identify a lack of focus in personalization
3. If many selling points are stacked together without three main themes, return to narrowing them to three USPs. Do not force quantification when numbers are unavailable

## Calculations and interpretation

Use only verifiable product differences. Mark claims lacking evidence as needing support; do not generate fabricated evidence

## Verification cases

**MK-014-C05 | The specification is true, but no industry comparison data is available**

Expected: Functional benefits may be proposed; do not claim the product is unique or number one

**MK-014-C06 | The information consists only of technical terms**

Expected: Explain their role under the known usage conditions first; do not invent unverified effects
