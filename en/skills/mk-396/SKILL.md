# MK-396 | Dormant Member Reactivation Copy

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-396.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Real benefits, reasons to return, and opt-out choices**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Current member segments/dormancy definition, contact eligibility, product benefits/evidence, complete approved offer conditions, destination link, channel and actual opt-out mechanism

## Method steps

1. Verify dormancy using current lists/brand rules; exclude returned buyers or ineligible contacts
2. Choose a real product benefit or approved offer and preserve amount, window and eligibility
3. Write message text, CTA and the brand’s actual opt-out route without inventing personal reasons
4. Check offer, links and opt-out flow; deliver the draft and eligible segment

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
