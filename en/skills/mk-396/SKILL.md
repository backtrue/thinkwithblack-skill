# MK-396 | Dormant Member Reactivation Copy

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-396.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Real benefits, reasons to return, and opt-out choices**

Customer segmentation and actual eligibility to receive marketing messages are separate matters

## Required inputs

Provide: member groups, product cycles, available offers, and permission to receive messages

## Method steps

1. Stronger incentives can be used to reactivate dormant members
2. Course examples include generous shopping credits and flash sales; SMS is recommended only for major occasions
3. Before reactivation, verify dormancy against the latest RFM list to avoid repeatedly sending strong offers to customers who have already repurchased
4. The brand determines actual discounts and credit amounts; the skill does not set them itself

## Calculation and interpretation

Base repeat purchase reminders on purchase cycles and contact consent; this catalog does not actually send messages
