# MK-280｜Video Opening Hook Diagnosis

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-280.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Unclear or irrelevant sections at the beginning**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Without evidence of reading the video or audio, provide recommendations only for the available text; do not invent video content

## Required Inputs

Readable video or timecoded transcript, media task, viewer context, brand/product facts; destination for CTA checks

## Method Steps

1. Record visible/audible opening content by timecode and when its relevance becomes clear
2. Check specificity of the problem, benefit or trigger, unnecessary preamble and missing context
3. Write an alternative opening and visual/audio brief tied to observations

## Calculation and Interpretation

Assess only observed text, images and sound; revisions are hypotheses, not demonstrated performance improvements
