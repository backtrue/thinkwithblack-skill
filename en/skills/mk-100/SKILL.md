# MK-100 | Warm-up phase task design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-100.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Arrangements for gathering needs, reminders, and education**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. Warm-up aims to accumulate digital assets that can convert before the campaign, rather than exposure alone
2. Prioritize mechanisms that record Purchase events, first-party contacts, VC, ATC, or LINE OA contacts; exposure alone is a weaker option
3. Warm-up formats can include giveaways, surveys, reservations, or preorder vouchers, with a small marketing budget and a user-sharing component
4. The contacts/events generated during warm-up must be usable for remarketing or conversion during the main campaign

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
