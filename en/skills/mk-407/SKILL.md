# MK-407 | LINE Copy by Customer Status

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-407.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Copy for different customer statuses within the same campaign**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Newly joined non-buyers: education + first-purchase reasons; product viewers who have not purchased: pain-point reinforcement + trust; purchasers: usage guidance + repeat purchase
2. High-value members: new products / exclusive offers; people inactive for a long time: reactivation or cleanup
3. The friend list should not all receive one identical message; move people to the appropriate segment when their status changes

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
