# MK-149｜Extract Authentic Review Highlights

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-149.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Customer review highlights that preserve the original meaning**

Keep review excerpts faithful; a recommendation, collaboration plan, or reuse arrangement does not mean the other party has been contacted

## Required Inputs

Provide: actual reviews, customer service records, collaboration content, permissions, and performance data

## Method Steps

1. Find usable testimonials in authentic user reviews, Page/business profile comments, sales page testimonial sections, and authorized third-party content
2. Extract specific usage experiences, outcome descriptions, or common questions that answer trust concerns, rather than only five-star adjectives
3. Preserve the original source; do not rewrite reviews as the brand's own claims

## Calculation and Interpretation

Use only authentic reviews and third-party content with usage rights; do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, or engagement
