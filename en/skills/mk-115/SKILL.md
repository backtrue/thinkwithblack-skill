# MK-115 | Phasing the campaign budget

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-115.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Objectives and budget assumptions for each phase**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Split the budget into warm-up, main campaign, and repeat-purchase phases rather than spreading it evenly
2. The course's reference pacing is approximately 4% for warm-up, heavy investment in the first three main-campaign days, steady spending in the middle, approximately 20% in the last three days, and approximately 1% for repeat purchases. The slides explicitly label these reference values
3. Derive actual amounts from the contribution margin table, break-even revenue, expected revenue, target bundles sold, and traffic first; do not simply apply percentages

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
