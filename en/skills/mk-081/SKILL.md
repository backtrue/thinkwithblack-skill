# MK-081 | Selecting high-margin products

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-081.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Products with a profit-generating role and their cost constraints**

Products occurring in the same transaction do not establish psychological motives; a product pairing does not automatically prove cross-selling effectiveness

## Required inputs

Provide: SKUs, product costs, customer groups, purchase records, and product lines

## Method

1. Use high-margin products at the repeat-purchase stage to generate greater gross-profit contribution from customers who already trust the brand
2. Do not select solely on selling price; verify profitability with actual costs/contribution margin ratio
3. Connect high-margin products with traffic-driving, trial, and core products; do not isolate them as expensive items without an audience source

## Calculations and interpretation

Product selections and next-need proposals are candidates; check inventory, gross margin, existing buyers, and sales data
