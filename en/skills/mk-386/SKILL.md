# MK-386 | High-Value Member Selection

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-386.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Candidates assessed jointly on frequency, amount, and recency**

Do not rank RFM results from different observation periods as if they shared one basis; separately verify the purpose and authorization for any list export or use

## Required inputs

Provide: de-identified customer, order, date, amount, and refund data

## Method steps

1. First use RFM Total Score to identify high-value candidates such as loyal and high-potential members
2. The course materials require at least filtering loyal members and loyal members due for reactivation; loyal members can be invited into a small VIP space
3. High value is more than one high order value; return to the R, F, M combination and the brand's member classification rules

## Calculation and interpretation

First define members, valid orders, refunds, and the period; calculate frequency using orders rather than item rows
