# MK-127｜Sales Page Offer Comparison

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-127.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **A comparison of bundles, prices, and intended users**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. In the eighth cell, present current promotions or multiple purchase bundles; the aim is to give people already interested in buying a higher-value option, beyond a single item
2. Present multiple quantities, multi-product bundles, or the month's promotional bundles so consumers can compare and increase average order value
3. Prices and offers must reflect actual purchasable terms, and must be checked against the contribution margin rate

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication

## Verification Cases

**MK-127-C05｜Comparison periods have different lengths or definitions**

Expected: do not directly compare totals; first list items that are not comparable and those that can safely be compared
