# MK-239｜Turn Search Terms into FAQs

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-239.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **FAQ proposals based on real search questions**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. Turn problem/solution search terms into FAQ questions users would actually ask
2. Answer the original search question and lead to a reasonable next step, rather than merely inserting keywords into Q&A
3. Prioritize frequent questions that relate to the purchase path and remain unanswered on current pages

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
