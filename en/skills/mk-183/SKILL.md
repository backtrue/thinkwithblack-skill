# MK-183｜Cart-Stage Concern-Resolution Copy

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-183.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Copy addressing shipping, payment, and risk**

Event groups do not necessarily represent the same people dropping off at each stage; verify counting and attribution across data sources

## Required Inputs

Actual cart/checkout flow, disclosed charges for that checkout, shipping/payment/delivery conditions, error/service evidence and product facts; actual offer rules when used

## Method Steps

1. For cart adders who have not purchased, prioritize the shopping process, shipping/unexpected costs, and whether completing the order now is worthwhile
2. Use new-customer credit, shipping credit, or starter bundles only when the brand actually offers them
3. The copy should remove barriers to completing the purchase rather than repeat top-of-funnel product introductions

## Calculation and Interpretation

Verify source periods, audience overlap, and exclusions; funnel changes alone do not prove psychological causes
