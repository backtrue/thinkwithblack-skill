# MK-108 | Gift-with-purchase threshold planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-108.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Calculations of average order value, gift cost, and spending thresholds**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. A gift-with-purchase threshold aims to increase average order value; the classic anniversary-campaign flow also uses threshold gifts to raise AOV
2. Include gift costs in the contribution margin table; do not look only at revenue growth
3. The original course material provides no fixed spending-threshold formula; design the threshold using average order value, gross margin, and the affordable gift cost

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
