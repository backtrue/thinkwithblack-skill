# MK-410 | Friend Lifetime Value Estimate

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-410.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Estimates with explicit periods, gross margins, and scenario assumptions**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. The course materials provide a simplified LTV formula: purchases in one year × average order value × gross margin rate
2. Reassess affordable CPF through an LTV lens, rather than only looking at ROAS on the joining date
3. This is a simplified estimate; if the brand has fuller retention, repeat purchase, and cost models, use actual data rather than only the simplified formula

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
