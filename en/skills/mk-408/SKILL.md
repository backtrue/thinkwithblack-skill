# MK-408 | Cost per Friend Calculation

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-408.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **A consistent basis for spending and valid newly added friends**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. CPF = LAP advertising spend ÷ newly added friends
2. CPF is only the entry cost of obtaining permission for continued communication, rather than the final outcome
3. Use advertising spend and newly added friends from the same period; do not force a cost calculation when there are zero new friends

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales

## Verification cases

**MK-408-A1 | Same-period LAP spend100 and ten new friends**

Expected: CPF = 10, retaining acquisition definition/window

**MK-408-A2 | Zero new friends**

Expected: CPF undefined, not zero cost
