# MK-267｜Demand Generation Task Definition

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-267.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **The business question this campaign needs to test**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Views or clicks do not automatically stand in for sales; do not directly rank channels measured on different bases

## Required Inputs

Business goal, need triggers, product/page capabilities, available evidence and resources

## Method Steps

1. Translate “run Demand Gen” into a business question with audience, need and desired behavior
2. Write a falsifiable hypothesis from evidence and separate business outcomes from intermediate views/clicks
3. Specify data source, window, brand decision conditions and next action; leave absent criteria open while delivering the question/hypothesis

## Calculation and Interpretation

The specified observations must answer the question; click data alone supports click-related conclusions
