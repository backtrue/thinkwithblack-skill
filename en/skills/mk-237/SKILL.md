# MK-237｜Find Content Opportunities in Search Terms

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-237.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Topics suited to content but not necessarily paid traffic**

If the account cannot show all terms, do not call the data complete market demand; the same term can mean different things in different service contexts

## Required Inputs

Provide: search terms, keywords, campaign performance, pages, and demand hypotheses

## Method Steps

1. Find new demand, customer language, pain points, and common questions in search terms
2. Mark terms with demand that current pages do not answer as content opportunities, feeding them into SEO, FAQ, sales pages, or product pages
3. Separate content opportunities from increased ad investment; a term may deserve content without being suitable for directly purchased ad traffic

## Calculation and Interpretation

Search term reports may not cover the entire market; observe when evidence is insufficient, and do not choose negative keywords from guesses alone
