# MK-427 | UTM Naming Plan

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-427.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Rules for source, medium, campaign, and creative**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Provide: Channels/platforms, campaign naming requirements, current source/medium/campaign dictionary and examples; performance exports are not required for naming

## Method steps

1. The five UTM fields map as follows: source=source, medium=medium, campaign=advertising campaign, content=manual ad creative, term=manual term
2. The course materials require source, medium, and campaign; lowercase English is recommended for source and medium, while campaign may use Chinese for easier recognition
3. Naming should distinguish new / returning customers, organic / paid traffic, and partner sources, with consistency across the team

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify data sources, dates, currencies, denominators, and attribution; sums across platforms do not equal unique reach or incremental impact
