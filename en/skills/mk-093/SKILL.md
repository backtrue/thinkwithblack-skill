# MK-093 | Organizing high- and low-season pacing

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-093.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Seasonality hypotheses grounded in historical data**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Annual goals, historical seasonality, brand events, capacity, and budget

## Method

1. Identify high/low seasons from brand history or industry data first, recognizing that revenue will not be the same every month
2. Concentrate on capturing available revenue in peak months; use less profitable months for content, educational traffic, or developing corresponding products
3. Connect seasonality findings to monthly revenue targets and budgets rather than merely labeling months

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
