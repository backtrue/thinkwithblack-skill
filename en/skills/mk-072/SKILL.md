# MK-072 | Breaking down traffic, lead, and order acquisition costs

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-072.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Numerators, denominators, and calculations for the three costs**

Recalculability does not mean the data is complete; break-even points and cost ceilings apply only under the stated assumptions

## Required inputs

Provide: Spend by source for a common period, corresponding valid traffic, leads and orders, and new/existing customer definitions

For method questions, provide the formula, variables and an example directly; for a calculation, ask only for missing operands

## Method

1. Separate three costs: traffic acquisition cost, lead acquisition cost, and order acquisition cost
2. Further split traffic and orders into new/existing customers and organic/paid sources so different acquisition methods are not combined into one cost
3. Use the same period and corresponding denominator for each cost; do not force a calculation with zero leads or orders

## Calculations and interpretation

Traffic cost = corresponding spend ÷ corresponding traffic; lead cost = corresponding spend ÷ valid leads; order cost = corresponding spend ÷ valid orders

Retain original values, units, periods, and substituted formulas. The formula is not an unconditional guarantee of results

Define gross profit, contribution margin, cost denominators, and periods explicitly; do not copy classroom example numbers as universal thresholds
