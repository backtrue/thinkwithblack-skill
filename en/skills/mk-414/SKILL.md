# MK-414 | Concise SMS Draft

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-414.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Actual campaign conditions and the required action**

Do not send messages or create automation without approval; if a tool cannot show recipient eligibility, do not assume everyone can be messaged

## Required inputs

Provide: friend or subscriber status, permissions, product content, campaigns, and channel reports

## Method steps

1. Prioritize brevity for SMS; the course materials keep the Chinese SMS copy structure within approximately 70 characters
2. A shared structure across placements can start with a question in the first sentence, answer it in the second, then end with a shortened URL and CTA
3. All offers, deadlines, and member benefits must come from the actual campaign; do not invent them
4. SMS is an interruptive mobile channel; verify the contactable list and current segmentation before sending

## Calculation and interpretation

Verify contact consent, unsubscribes, and message specifications; friend counts and open rates are not sales
