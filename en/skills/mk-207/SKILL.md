# MK-207｜New-Customer Hero Post Assessment

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-207.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Performance and suitable audiences for new-customer acquisition**

Past success supports candidates and hypotheses only; shared content elements are not proven causes of success

## Required Inputs

Provide: Post IDs, cross-audience performance, creative, and seed-list quality

## Method Steps

1. For an acquisition Hero Post, prioritize whether it maintains strong CTR and engagement in traffic/new-customer audiences and shows downstream ATC/conversion signals
2. Test the same post across different new-customer audiences to validate scalability
3. With insufficient samples, list it only as a candidate; one high-CTR result does not establish a Hero Post

## Calculation and Interpretation

Aggregate by Post ID and distinguish advertising contexts; high engagement or high ROAS alone does not guarantee scalability
