# MK-355 | Cross-Platform Content Adaptation Plan

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-355.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **One topic adapted to each platform's task, rather than spun content**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Original content/core viewpoint, approved brand facts/sources, target platforms and audiences, intended action and available assets

## Method steps

1. Adapt one core viewpoint into digital assets suited to external contexts: forum Q&A / experience, blogger reviews and comparisons, media viewpoints and trends, YouTube / Reels demonstrations, and short social summaries
2. Adaptation preserves the core viewpoint while changing to a suitable platform format, rather than copying and pasting the original
3. Every asset must still address the same topic and accurate brand context

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
