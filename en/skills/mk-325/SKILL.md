# MK-325 | Article Subheading Clarity Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-325.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Subheadings that remain unclear outside their context**

General content rewriting differs from Speak Human TW language review; do not automatically apply its review documents without an explicit trigger

## Required inputs

Provide: the full article, reader questions, sources, and actual experience or evidence

## Method steps

1. Make H2 headings resemble real user questions so that readers can tell what a section answers from its heading alone
2. Avoid headings consisting only of keywords or internal categories such as “Product features / More information”
3. Each H2 should address one search need and remain understandable without its surrounding context

## Calculation and interpretation

Check only presence and clarity; do not invent experts, experience, or data, or guarantee AI citations
