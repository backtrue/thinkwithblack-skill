# MK-041 | Ad-to-page promise check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-041.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Breaks where the page does not answer the ad's promise**

Task stages are analytical categories, not a claim that every customer follows the same path

## Required inputs

Provide: Actual search terms and sources, ad text and promises, landing page content or URL, supported products/services and action

## Method

1. Compare whether the search term, ad promise, and landing page's first screen/main content answer the same question
2. A click only means someone arrived. Successful continuity means they immediately see an answer to their previous question and a reasonable next step
3. If promises are inconsistent, fix the ad or page before increasing traffic

## Calculations and interpretation

Search phrases and paths are planning hypotheses; check them against real search terms and evidence of how pages support the need
