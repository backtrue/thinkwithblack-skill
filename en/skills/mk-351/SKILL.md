# MK-351 | Third-Party Testimonial Source Inventory

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-351.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Media coverage, cases, and usable third-party statements**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Inventory third-party mentions in media, KOL content, blogs, YouTube, partner content, reviews, comparisons, recommendation lists, and similar sources
2. Beyond traffic / clicks, check whether they explain who the brand is, whom it suits, product uses, comparisons, and contexts
3. Mentions consisting only of a photo / logo / brand name without context have less value as external testimony

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
