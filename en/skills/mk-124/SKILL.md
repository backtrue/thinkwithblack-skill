# MK-124｜Feature Evidence Section

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-124.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Modules pairing features, explanations, and real evidence**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Provide: product facts, Persona, selling points, authentic evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method Steps

1. Cells four–six should do more than list features: explain how an ingredient or feature solves the earlier pain point or brings about the desirable future described earlier
2. Product features must matter to the Persona; specifications unrelated to the user do not belong among the three main points
3. If the three most important points are unknown, the course recommends a questionnaire-based warm-up campaign in which consumers vote, rather than relying solely on the brand's guesses

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
