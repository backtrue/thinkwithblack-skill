# MK-094 | Monthly theme planning

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-094.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Monthly themes connecting needs, Personas, and products**

Historical seasonality can inform planning but does not guarantee recurrence; do not create paid schedules or publish without user approval

## Required inputs

Provide: Monthly goal and actual need, product price and margin, Persona, USP, seasonal/campaign evidence, capacity and budget; annual data only for existing annual dependencies

## Method

1. Combine key annual events, that month's essential needs, the target Persona's pain points/values, and product USPs into each monthly marketing theme
2. The theme should answer "Why buy this month?", not simply place a holiday name in front of a product
3. AI can help generate themes, but inputs must include product pricing/gross margin, the target month's essential needs, Persona, and USPs. Do not force theme generation with insufficient data

## Calculations and interpretation

Use actual brand data for campaign dates and high/low seasons; classroom schedules are not fixed market laws
