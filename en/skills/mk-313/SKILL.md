# MK-313｜Hub Page Module Design

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-313.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Required answers, evidence, and referral sections**

Permission in robots or the existence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine site technical status and content quality into a single conclusion

## Required Inputs

Provide: website pages, HTML, existing content, audiences, and search demand

## Method Steps

1. Build hub pages from independently understandable chunks: a question-based H2, a short answer-first paragraph, then lists/tables/steps as needed
2. Different modules on one page address questions at different readiness levels and clearly direct readers onward
3. Each chunk must answer one question independently; do not mix all answers into one large paragraph

## Calculation and Interpretation

Actually crawl/read content and site structure; check current rules and markup, without promising rankings or citations
