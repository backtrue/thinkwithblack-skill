# MK-450 | Consulting Output Acceptance Questions

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-450.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Real case inputs and conditions for an acceptable result**

Maintain, observe, and gather evidence first are valid decisions; do not conceal a lack of evidence behind a well-organized report

## Required inputs

Provide: brand background, existing data, course rules, and the current decision

## Method steps

1. Acceptance at the Do stage: required data, pages / modules to change, who provides data, and how completion will be checked
2. Acceptance at the Check stage: completed / insufficient items, changes in AI answers, changes potentially related to optimization, remaining problems, and adjustments to the next Plan
3. Validate the Plan rather than generating another question bank; do not attribute changes to optimization without evidence

## Calculation and interpretation

This task only inventories work; old specifications are not new authorization, and tools must actually be available before claiming execution
