# MK-031 | Next needs of existing buyers

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-031.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Testable hypotheses about a buyer's subsequent needs**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Start with the purchased role-marker product and work back to shared audience characteristics
2. Find follow-on products through two routes: other products needed by people with the same audience characteristics, or products extending the same product characteristics
3. Confirm needs first using real shopping carts, customer/community questions, and essential accessories in the industry, rather than guessing solely through ads

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior

## Verification cases

**MK-031-C05 | An order contains maternity and baby products**

Expected: Do not immediately identify the buyer as a mother; separate the buying and using roles

Expected: Keep both candidates and request the data that would distinguish them
