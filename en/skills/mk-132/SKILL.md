# MK-132｜Sales Page Action Entry Check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-132.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Alignment between offers, buttons, and page goals**

A page content check does not replace user behavior data; without performance data, do not assert the cause of declining conversions

## Required Inputs

Actual page/CTA, intended purchase action, product/offer conditions, links and verifiable stock/status

## Method Steps

1. The third row of the nine-cell framework must provide a clear reason to buy and a way to act; do not expect an add-to-cart action without explaining why to buy now
2. Bundles and promotions in the eighth cell must connect directly to the purchase action so interested buyers can select a more suitable bundle
3. The CTA's offer, deadline, link, and product status must be genuine and usable

## Calculation and Interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials require original support; produce page drafts only, without automatic publication
