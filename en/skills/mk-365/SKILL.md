# MK-365 | Comparison of Brands Listed Together by AI

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-365.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Brands appearing together and their context**

One answer does not establish a stable ranking; do not simulate current results when actual answers have not been obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: actual AI answers, queries, dates, platforms, and cited URLs

## Method steps

1. Record co-mentioned brands, full prompt, platform, date, answer and citations
2. Treat co-mention as an observation for this run; identify possible pages/external descriptions and leave unattributed causes open
3. Compare actual sources with approved positioning; propose specific corrections when sources differ or reproducible observations have supporting evidence
4. Recheck under comparable conditions and retain non-reproductions; a single co-mention does not by itself justify site changes

## Calculation and interpretation

Retain questions, dates, platforms, and actual answers; one result does not represent answers shown to all users
