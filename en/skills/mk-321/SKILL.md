# MK-321 | Page Image Alt Text Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-321.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Missing information about image content and alternative text**

Permission in robots or the presence of a sitemap does not guarantee indexing; do not combine technical website status and content quality into one conclusion

## Required inputs

Provide: website pages, HTML, existing content, audience, and search needs

## Method steps

1. Check whether important images have img alt text that explains their meaning
2. Alt text helps machines understand images; describe the actual image and its role on the page
3. Do not use alt text for keyword stuffing or describe information absent from the image

## Calculation and interpretation

Actually crawl and read the content and website structure; check current rules and markup, without guaranteeing rankings or citations
