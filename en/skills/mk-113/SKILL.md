# MK-113 | Limited-time offer truthfulness check

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-113.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Verification of dates, inventory, and offer promises**

Do not fabricate discounts, gift inventory, countdown deadlines, limited quantities, or testimonials

## Required inputs

Provide: Campaign goals, products, costs, real offer conditions, and customer groups

## Method

1. Use limited-time/limited-quantity messages mainly on campaign pages and during countdowns to accelerate conversion among engaged or considering audiences
2. Countdown creative must match actual campaign dates; quantity limits require real quantities or conditions
3. Product pages provide ongoing information; do not present permanently available products as continuously time-limited promotions

## Calculations and interpretation

Check gross margin and stacked offers; time limits, inventory, and benefits must be real, with no promise of guaranteed results
