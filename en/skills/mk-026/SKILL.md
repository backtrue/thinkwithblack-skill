# MK-026 | Occasion-based scenario angles

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-026.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Differences in needs for gifting, everyday use, and special occasions**

Hypotheses are useful but do not establish demographics or market share; do not fabricate interview content or customer experiences

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, existing customer data, surveys, or customer-service records

## Method

1. Use Occasion in T.P.O. to identify festivals, events, or life situations that increase the target audience's willingness to pay
2. Return to the annual planning table and connect the situation with product bundles, prices, and reasons for communication
3. The purpose of a scenario is to identify the value an audience prioritizes in that context, not to discount at every holiday

## Calculations and interpretation

Treat customer roles and motivations as hypotheses initially; do not establish someone's actual identity from a single purchase behavior
