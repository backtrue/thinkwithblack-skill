# MK-053 | Launch-price and early-bird-price questionnaire

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-053.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Questions measuring acceptance of two offers**

Without raw survey responses, produce only a questionnaire or analysis plan; do not fabricate answers

## Required inputs

Provide: Respondents, actual product/bundle, launch and early-bird prices and conditions, scale preference; costs and brand floor for feasibility, not existing responses as a prerequisite for question design

## Method

1. A simplified price questionnaire first explains the product/bundle characteristics fully, then asks about acceptance of the planned launch price and early-bird launch price
2. The course example first requires the planned launch price to maintain a higher contribution margin ratio and the early-bird price to retain a floor ratio, then tests acceptance
3. The survey can use a 1–7-star acceptance scale, but actual prices and bundles must come from real cost planning

## Calculations and interpretation

Check sampling, valid responses, and the ordering of price questions; willingness to pay is neither actual purchasing nor a profit guarantee
