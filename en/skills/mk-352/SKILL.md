# MK-352 | Third-Party Content Improvement Priorities

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-352.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Priorities based on gaps, impact, and ability to act**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Use search results for core questions / keywords to inventory external context pages where the brand should appear: recommendation lists, comparisons, question roundups, industry features, videos, and similar content
2. Record whether the brand is currently mentioned, how it should accurately be mentioned, and high / medium / low outreach priority
3. Do not contact every page; start with important pages likely to shape answer context where brand positioning is wrong or the brand is absent

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
