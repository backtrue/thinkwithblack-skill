# MK-419 | Product Education Post Plan

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-419.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Questions and visuals readers need to understand**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Start product education with the month's campaign products and consumer needs, organizing it into an understandable educational resource
2. Go beyond specifications to help potential new customers or F1 members understand use, selection, or reasons for the next purchase
3. Content can be incorporated into member newsletters so social content and member communication share the same topic assets

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
