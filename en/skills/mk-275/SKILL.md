This Skill delivers: **Groups, audiences, creative, goals, and budget**

Execution steps compiled by ThinkWithBlack for this method's stated purpose, 2026-10-21; the course outline and original sources are retained below

Views or clicks do not automatically stand in for sales; do not directly rank channels measured on different bases

## Required Inputs

Goal, audience/creative candidates, pages, total budget/deadline, production and fulfillment capacity

## Method Steps

1. Draft campaigns/groups around the business question, mapping audience, creative, destination and outcome
2. Schedule asset readiness, measurement and review dependencies
3. Reconcile group budgets/reserve/capacity and specify decision points and adjustment evidence

## Calculation and Interpretation

Group allocations plus reserve must fit the total; a draft is not a created campaign
