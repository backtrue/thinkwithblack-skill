# MK-425 | Social Content Referral Check

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-425.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Alignment between post promises and linked pages**

High reach supports observations about content, not paid conversion performance; do not claim to have viewed visuals you have not actually obtained

## Required inputs

Provide: monthly themes, existing assets, real customer feedback, and content performance

## Method steps

1. Social content should go beyond exposure to move non-followers, potential new customers, or F1 members toward the next action
2. Check that the post has a clear product / content / member entry point consistent with this month's marketing theme
3. Destinations may include the official website, LINE OA, member events, or another established entry point; the original course materials do not require every post to point to one page

## Calculation and interpretation

Content reuse requires fidelity and permission; assess reach, engagement, clicks, and sales separately
