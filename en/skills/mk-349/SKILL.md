# MK-349 | Incorrect Brand Description Verification

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-349.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Incorrect content, original evidence, and correction drafts**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Collect descriptions of the brand from AI, media, KOLs, partners, and external pages
2. Compare them with approved brand positioning; find errors in target audiences, product categories, price level / positioning, and differentiators
3. Incorrect external context can be more troublesome than no mention; prioritize descriptions that place the brand in the wrong category

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained
