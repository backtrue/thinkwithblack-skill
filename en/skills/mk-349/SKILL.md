# MK-349 | Incorrect Brand Description Verification

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-349.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Incorrect content, original evidence, and correction drafts**

Brand mentions, citations, and correct descriptions are different outcomes and cannot substitute for one another

## Required inputs

Provide: brand information, the official website, off-site mentions, media coverage, and search results

## Method steps

1. Preserve the original off-site statement/content, query or context, and source; compare each claim with approved brand facts
2. Locate errors, overreach and missing information, and prioritize their impact
3. Write the complete corrected draft using supported facts and conditions, retaining unknowns for confirmation
4. Check the corrected draft sentence by sentence against sources and deliver original/revised wording with evidence

## Calculation and interpretation

Use official original information as the basis for brand facts; creating materials or requesting corrections does not mean a citation has been obtained

## Required output fields

| Order | Field | How to complete |
|---:|---|---|

| 2 | Approved brand facts and sources | Use the supplied source, calculation, or explicit unknown |
| 3 | Difference and supported scope | Use the supplied source, calculation, or explicit unknown |
| 4 | Correction priority | Use the supplied source, calculation, or explicit unknown |

| 6 | Fact-by-fact check | Use the supplied source, calculation, or explicit unknown |
| 7 | Open facts | Use the supplied source, calculation, or explicit unknown |
