# MK-138｜Situational Question Opening

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-138.md`; it includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This Skill delivers: **Opening candidates matched to usage occasions**

Creating a draft from scratch here does not automatically invoke Speak Human TW; when editing an existing draft, read its original instructions only if that proofreading need clearly applies

## Required Inputs

Provide: product, audience hypothesis, evidence, landing page, and placement requirements

## Method Steps

1. Build the opening question around the Who, When, Where, and What situations the Persona actually cares about
2. The question should help the Persona recognize themselves, rather than relying on a generic pain point unrelated to them
3. The product's USP/FABE must address what follows the question; do not ask an attention-grabbing question the product cannot solve

## Calculation and Interpretation

First verify product facts and creative usage rights; check current platform character counts, aspect ratios, and restrictions when producing the creative
