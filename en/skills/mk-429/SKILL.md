# MK-429 | KOL Collaboration UTM Classification

For ordinary chat, upload `packs/mk-429.md`, which includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **Identification of partners, placements, products, and collaborations**

Adding impressions across platforms does not yield deduplicated reach; conversions with different attribution windows cannot be added as actual sitewide orders

## Required inputs

Partner, platform/placement, product/bundle, campaign purpose, existing UTM dictionary/examples; existing links for review

## Method steps

1. For KOL / KOC collaborations, name utm_campaign first, then use source / medium to distinguish partner media and placements
2. The course materials suggest using optional term for target keywords and content for publication time; campaign may follow “product category_blogger name_sales bundle”
3. Naming supports future comparisons of partners and campaign value; do not fill fields with meaningless values merely for completeness

## Calculation and interpretation

Interpret source, medium, campaign, content and applicable term for each partner link and verify a unique campaign/creative mapping; report collisions/unknowns rather than fill meaningless values
