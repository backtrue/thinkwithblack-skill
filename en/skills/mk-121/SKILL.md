# MK-121 | Pain-point opening for a sales page

For a regular chat, upload `packs/mk-121.md`. It includes the shared rules, this method, and required references

## Purpose

This skill delivers: **An opening grounded in original customer needs**

A content review cannot replace user-behavior data; without performance data, do not assert why conversions declined

## Required inputs

Provide: Product facts, Persona, selling points, real evidence, offers, and brand information

## Method

1. In section 2 of the nine-section framework, use Problem/Implication logic to help the Persona recognize their pain points, obstacles, and costs
2. Develop pain points around time, money, relationships, shortcomings in existing solutions, or competitor shortcomings
3. Not every product needs fear-based amplification of pain. If a positive future better fits the Persona, use an ideal scenario instead

## Calculations and interpretation

Quotes, numbers, effects, and testimonials need original support; produce only a page draft and do not publish automatically
