# One product, three consulting tasks

This is a fictional exercise. For each task, upload INDEX.md, the selected method pack and the data below. After finishing one task, select the next method and obtain its complete text before switching

## Reproducible inputs

- P1 product specification: Everyday Cup C500, 500 ml, 304 stainless-steel body, silicone lid, 280 g, TWD 790; hand-wash only, not dishwasher safe
- P2 fictional competitor specification: Daily Cup D500, 500 ml, 304 stainless-steel body, plastic lid, 320 g, TWD 690; hand-wash only
- U1 brand brief: “For this task, consider people who carry water outside every day. No discounts. Suggest candidates using the existing specifications; I do not know which feature to prioritize”
- C1 claim to inspect: “The industry’s lightest, completely leakproof, high-quality travel cup”

P1 and P2 compare these two fictional cups only. Leak or insulation tests, reviews, demand research, whole-market comparisons and campaign results are unavailable and remain unknown

## Three prompts to copy

| User request | Expected method | Inputs and deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Turn P1 into citable specifications, unsupported claims and missing evidence | MK-001 Product Fact Organization | Use P1 and C1; deliver sourced product facts and gaps before choosing a lead selling point |
| Use P1, P2 and U1 to propose three evidence-backed selling-point candidates | MK-002 Unique Selling Point Extraction | Deliver factual feature, customer job, perceived benefit, comparison evidence, suitable angle, lead/support/investigate classification and reason; keep inferred benefits and unproven uniqueness visible |
| Use P1, P2 and U1 to check which parts of C1 could also describe a competitor | MK-003 USP Uniqueness Check | Deliver the original claim; exclusivity, personalization and quantification checks; generic wording; sourced differences; revised claim and evidence gaps |

Checkable result: P1 and P2 list a weight difference of 40 g. “40 g lighter than this comparison model” has evidence in the exercise. “Industry’s lightest” needs whole-market evidence, “completely leakproof” needs testing, and “high-quality” gives no verifiable difference. Keep those claims unproven

## Continuing after “I don’t know”

In the second task, reuse the known audience and no-discount constraint. Three candidates can be presented: 40 g less than P2, the silicone-lid material difference, and the 500 ml capacity. The first has comparison data; customer benefits and uniqueness of the other two still need evidence

A useful recommendation is: “Test the weight difference first. P1 and P2 provide comparable figures, and it fits U1’s outdoor-use direction. The angle remains a hypothesis. If you choose it, I will organize its comparison evidence and open checks, retaining other features as supporting or unverified candidates”

When the user says “I don’t know,” deliver the candidate comparison and a specific evidence action, such as asking the brand to verify measured weight and comparison conditions. Complete the candidate draft when the method permits it. Ask “Test the weight difference first, or collect evidence for another feature?” with the recommendation only when a subsequent step actually requires that business choice

## Corrections and a new conversation
