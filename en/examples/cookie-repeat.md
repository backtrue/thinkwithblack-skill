# First repeat-purchase practice

Everything below is a hypothetical scenario

- Product: handmade cookie gift boxes at NT$600 per box, available in original and cocoa flavors
- Main needs: personal snacks and gifts; purchase motivations have not been supported by interviews
- Audience: customers with exactly one valid paid order in the complete records from last year through the end of this month
- There are 120 one-time customers; 80 have consented to receive brand emails
- Among customers who have already bought again, the median interval between the first and second orders is 35 days. This is an observation about repeat purchasers
- Available products: both original and cocoa are in stock; no discount will be offered in this campaign
- No messages have been sent, and there are no results for this repeat-purchase campaign yet

Plan the timing, message, product and results-tracking table for encouraging the first repeat purchase. State the basis for each recommendation and the assumptions to test
