# Fictional exercise: are the membership rules understandable?

These are an imaginary brand's existing rules, practice version 1. Turn them into a customer-facing explanation while preserving every benefit. Check that the meaning remains equivalent, and prepare comprehension questions with separate facilitator answers

## Original rules

R1: When paying for physical products, earn 1 point for every NT$10 of product value after discounts and points redemption, rounding down any remainder below NT$10. Shipping is excluded. Points are credited on the 30th day after pickup and can be used within 365 days from the credit date. They remain usable on the expiration date and expire the following day

R2: Redeem 100 points for NT$100, on physical products only. Product value before points redemption must be at least NT$1,000. Each redemption is limited to 50% of that amount and must use increments of 100 points. Discount codes cannot be combined with points redemption. Subscriptions and digital content neither earn points nor accept points redemption

R3: On a refund, use the amount originally paid for the returned products to recalculate the points earned by the remaining products, and reclaim the difference. If points have not yet been credited, adjust the pending points. If already credited, deduct from the available balance first. If insufficient, reduce the balance to 0 and record the remainder as points to be recovered. New points first repay that remainder, and redemption is suspended until it is fully repaid. All refund scenarios in this exercise involve no points redemption or discount codes

R4: A monthly subscription at NT$299 provides access to subscription videos during the paid period. Cancellation stops renewal for the next period. Access continues through the paid period and ends when that period expires. There is no option to skip a period

R5: A separately purchased NT$699 digital course provides permanent viewing access. Its content differs from subscription videos. Cancelling a subscription does not affect access to that purchased course, and the subscription does not include the separately sold course

## Check these scenarios

1. A customer pays NT$500 for physical products plus NT$60 shipping. How many points from this purchase are usable on day 29 and day 30 after pickup?
2. A customer has 500 available points. How much can be redeemed when physical-product value before redemption is NT$999 versus exactly NT$1,000?
3. Points from an NT$500 order have been credited. The customer returns NT$200 of products and currently has only 10 available points. How are the points handled?
4. A monthly subscription is paid through October 31 and cancelled on October 15. Can the customer view the separately purchased NT$699 course in November? What about subscription videos?
5. Can points be used on their expiration date and on the following day?
